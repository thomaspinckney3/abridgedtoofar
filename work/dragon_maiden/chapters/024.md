# ABOUT MAC FLYNN

A seductress of sensual words and a lover of paranormal plots, Mac Flynn is the alias of a writer on the run from distraction. She lives on the edge of sagebrush and sanity with her many crazy cats, and finds pleasure in writing thrilling paranormal stories filled with naughty fun and hilarious hijinks. She’s the author of numerous paranormal and fantasy series that weave suspense, adventure and a good joke into a one-of-a-kind experience.  You can learn more about her at MacFlynn.com .

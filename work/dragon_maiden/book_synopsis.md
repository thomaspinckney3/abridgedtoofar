Office worker Miriam is abducted from her apartment by Blake, a pig-faced shapeshifter in disguise, and his henchmen. She wakes bound in a truck with four other young women—Stephanie, Alex, Olivia, and Cindy—and is delivered to a medieval-style fortress and village reached via a Portal. At the High Castle they are told they are to be presented as potential Maidens to dragon lords during a ceremonial Choosing. During the presentation Miriam offers herself in frightened Stephanie’s place and is selected by the central lord, later named Xander, despite his adviser Renner’s objections. Taken to chambers, Miriam is branded by the attendant Darda with Xander’s crest in a Marking that binds her to him and lets him sense her. She escapes out the window at night, reaches a forest glen, and accidentally summons the Lady of the Pond, who bestows a gift tied to Miriam’s bond with her lord. Back at the castle Miriam meets Xander privately, sees his wings, learns he is a dragon shifter, and the two consummate their bond under the influence of his scent. Xander explains that Maidens come from Miriam’s world to refresh dragon bloodlines, that returning through the Portal after Marking is lethal, and that he seeks her trust rather than imprisonment. The next gathering brings breakfast with young lord Cayden and his Maiden Stephanie and a plan to show Miriam the Portal. Priests Apuleius and Jethro activate the carved stone arch, demonstrating that objects from the dragon realm shatter on its surface. A flight of Bestia Draconis attacks the site, Xander and Cayden transform and battle them, a captive monk is hurled into the Portal and disintegrates, and the fighting reaches the platform. Xander tackles a dragon that menaces Miriam, but she loses her footing and falls through the Portal, emerging back in her city at a junkyard. Miriam hides with her friend Heather, suffers burning in her shoulder mark and a vision of a gravely wounded Xander, and resolves to return. Using the glowing mark, she reopens the Portal, comes back, secures curative water from the Lady of the Pond, and administers it so Xander survives poisoning from silver-tipped talons. After recovering, Xander accepts Miriam’s decision to remain with him, and the book ends with their party—along with Cayden and Stephanie—preparing to depart High Castle by horseback for Xander’s domain.

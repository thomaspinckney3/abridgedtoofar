He glanced over his shoulder, a sly curve to his mouth. “What do you think?”

“Wow,” I breathed, stepping closer despite myself. The wing skin was warm and taut under my fingers; the bones beneath felt like living iron. “You’re… you’re really a dragon, aren’t you?”
“I consider myself only half dragon. My mother was a Maiden like yourself, and my father took her as his bride,” he revealed.
He shuddered, jaw tight.

“Are you all right?”

“The wings of a dragon are very sensitive,” he said. “It is not pain.”

I started to pull my hand away; his caught my chin, steadying my gaze against the heat in his eyes. A scent spilled into the air—soft as a memory: lilacs after rain, fresh-cut grass. My fear loosened. The scent wrapped me like a blanket, and so did he, arms and then wings enclosing me in warmth.

“W-what are you doing to me?” I whispered, furious at how quickly the blanket soothed. I tried to will myself to hate this creature, this dragon-man who had kidnapped me and made me his slave, but I couldn’t.
“What do you smell?” he murmured at my ear.

“Lilacs. And cut grass.”

“Beautiful,” he said. “Like you.”

A kiss at my neck, gentle as a promise. I trembled. “What’s happening to me?”

“Ssh. Enjoy the feeling.”

His winged embrace tightened. I felt like a caterpillar preparing for a long, comforting nap before emerging as a beautiful butterfly. The thin dress bunched at my hips; his hands learned the lines of me with slow certainty. Heat coiled low; my traitor body answered him. He lifted me as if I weighed nothing and laid me on the bed. In the dimness his eyes were embers.

He drew the gown over my head and flung it aside. I lay naked beneath his eyes. They consumed me like a man starved of food who had finally found his feast. His hands mapped me, patient, unhurried. When his fingers found the place that pulsed, thought unraveled.

“Your desire for me is strong,” he said, voice rough with restraint. “How badly do you want me?”

“Badly,” I managed.

“Will you allow me to take you as my own? To make you mine and no other?”

“Yes. Oh god, yes.”

His smile flashed the hint of teeth. “Then you will be mine until I release you.”

He joined with me. The world narrowed to motion and breath, the sure rhythm of him, the blazing arc that gathered and broke me apart. I cried out; he held me through it, following into his own shuddering end. After, a wing draped over me like a quilt, heavy and warm. He drew me close. Exhaustion pulled me under.

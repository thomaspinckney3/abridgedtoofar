Grey, misty dawn; sailors ran ahead.

“Avast!” cried a voice; a hand fell on our shoulders; a figure peered between us. Elijah.

“Going aboard?”

“Yes; and what’s that to you?”

“Coming back afore breakfast?” he wondered. Then, stealing after: “Did ye see anything like men going towards that ship a while ago?”

“I thought I saw four or five, but it was dim.”

“Very dim, very dim. See if you can find ’em now, will ye?—Morning to ye! morning to ye! Oh! I was going to warn ye against—but never mind, never mind—it’s all one, all in the family too;—sharp frost this morning, ain’t it? Good-bye to ye. Shan’t see ye again very soon, I guess; unless it’s before the Grand Jury.”

We stepped on board. Profound quiet; cabin locked; hatches on. In the forecastle, a tattered old rigger lay face-down across two chests, in profoundest sleep. We sat near—Queequeg, after feeling if the sleeper’s rear was soft enough, perched upon him in his “country way.”

“Gracious!—don’t sit there,” said I.
“Oh! perry dood seat,” said Queequeg; “my country way; won’t hurt him face.”
“Face!” said I; “call that his face?—you are heavy; it’s grinding the face of the poor. Get off, Queequeg! Look, he’ll twitch you off soon. I wonder he don’t wake.”
He shifted by the head, lit his tomahawk-pipe, and flourished its hatchet-side over the sleeper. We kept the pipe passing over him, from one to the other; and meanwhile, upon questioning him in his broken fashion, Queequeg gave me to understand that, in his land, the king, chiefs, and great people generally, were in the custom of fattening some of the lower orders for ottomans; and to furnish a house comfortably in that respect, you had only to buy up eight or ten lazy fellows, and lay them round in the piers and alcoves.

“What’s that for, Queequeg?”
“Perry easy, kill-e; oh! perry easy!”

The heavy vapor filled the hole; the man breathed muffledly, rolled, and sat up rubbing his eyes. “Holloa! who be ye smokers?”
“Shipped men. When does she sail?”
“She sails to-day. The Captain came aboard last night.”
“What Captain?—Ahab?”
“Who but him indeed?”
A noise on deck—“Holloa! Starbuck’s astir,” said the rigger; “good man, and a pious.” He turned to; we followed.

Clear sunrise now; the crew came aboard by twos and threes; riggers bestirred; mates engaged; shore-folk hurried last bundles. Meanwhile Captain Ahab remained invisibly enshrined within his cabin.

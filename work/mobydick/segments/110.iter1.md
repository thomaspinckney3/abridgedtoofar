Seat thyself sultanically among the moons of Saturn, and take high abstracted man alone; and he seems a wonder, a grandeur, and a woe. But take mankind in mass, and they are a mob of unnecessary duplicates. Yet the Pequod’s carpenter was no duplicate. Like all sea-going carpenters—especially in whalers—he was, to an off-handed, practical extent, experienced in numerous collateral trades; but singularly efficient, too, in those thousand nameless emergencies that befall a large ship on a three or four years’ voyage in far seas.

His grand stage was his vice-bench, a long, rude, ponderous table with several vices, iron and wood, lashed athwartships against the Try-works’ rear. A belaying pin too large for its hole—into the vice and filed smaller; a lost land-bird made captive—out of right-whale bone and sperm ivory he made a pagoda-looking cage. An oarsman sprains his wrist—he concocts a lotion. Stubb longs for vermillion stars upon his oar-blades—each oar screwed fast, the constellation supplied. A sailor with toothache—the carpenter out pincers; the carpenter whirls the handle of his wooden vice and signs him to clap his jaw in that, if he would have the tooth drawn. Shark-bone ear-rings? He drills the ears.

Prepared at all points, indifferent and without respect in all; teeth to him were bits of ivory; heads but top-blocks; men capstans. This might argue uncommon vivacity; but not precisely so. He was remarkable for a certain impersonal stolidity—impersonal, I say; for it so shaded off into the surrounding infinite of things, that it seemed one with the general stolidity discernible in the whole visible world; which while pauselessly active in uncounted modes, still eternally holds its peace, and ignores you, though you dig foundations for cathedrals. Yet was it oddly dashed at times, with an old, crutch-like, antediluvian, wheezing humorousness, not unstreaked now and then with a certain grizzled wittiness; such as might have served to pass the time during the midnight watch on the bearded forecastle of Noah’s ark. A life-long wanderer, a stript abstract; an unfractioned integral; uncompromised as a new-born babe; living without premeditated reference to this world or the next.

He did not work by reason, nor instinct, nor schooling, but by a deaf and dumb, spontaneous literal process. A pure manipulator; his brain, if ever he had one, must early have oozed into the muscles of his fingers. Like one of those unreasoning yet highly useful multum in parvo Sheffield contrivances—pocket-knife outwardly, but containing screw-drivers, cork-screws, tweezers, awls, pens, rulers, nail-filers, countersinkers. If superiors wanted a screw-driver, they opened that part of him and the screw was fast; if tweezers, took him up by the legs, and there they were. And yet some subtle life-principle—quicksilver or hartshorn—kept him a great part of the time soliloquizing; his body the sentry-box; the soliloquizer on guard.

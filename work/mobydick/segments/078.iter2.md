Cross the deck to the Right Whale’s head. If the Sperm’s front is a rounded Roman chariot, the Right’s bears an inelegant resemblance to a gigantic galliot-toed shoe; an old Dutch voyager likened it to a shoemaker’s last—ample enough to lodge that nursery old woman and all her brood. Nearer, the head shifts aspect: stand atop; the two F-shaped spoutholes make it an enormous bass-viol; fix on the green barnacled “crown” or “bonnet” with live crabs, and he is a diademed king—though a sulky one. Look at that hanging lower lip! what a huge sulk and pout is there! a sulk and pout, by carpenter’s measurement, about twenty feet long and five feet deep; a sulk and pout that will yield you some 500 gallons of oil and more.

A pity, now, that this whale should be hare-lipped—about a foot across. Probably the mother during an important interval was sailing down the Peruvian coast, when earthquakes caused the beach to gape. Over this lip, as over a slippery threshold, we now slide into the mouth. Were I at Mackinaw, I should take this to be the inside of an Indian wigwam. Good Lord! is this the road that Jonah went? The roof is about twelve feet high, and runs to a pretty sharp angle, as if there were a regular ridge-pole there; while these arched, hairy sides present those wondrous, half-vertical, scimetar-shaped slats—say three hundred on a side—depending from the crown-bone: Venetian blinds whose hairy fringes strain the seas of brit. Marks and hollows in the central blinds serve some to count age, as oaks by rings. Old travelers called them whiskers, hogs’ bristles, fins; and, indeed, the ladies’ busks draw their stiffening from the same bone. In Queen Anne’s time the bone was in its glory, the farthingale all the fashion; and as those ancient dames moved about gaily, though in the jaws of the whale, as you may say; even so, in a shower, with the like thoughtlessness, do we nowadays fly under the same jaws for protection; the umbrella being a tent spread over the same bone.

Now forget blinds and whiskers; standing within among colonnades of bone, would you not think you were inside of the great Haarlem organ, and gazing upon its thousand pipes? For a carpet to the organ we have a rug of the softest Turkey—the tongue—fat and tender; this one, at a glance, a six-barreler.

To sum up: no great well of sperm here; no ivory teeth; no long slender mandible; while in the Sperm there are none of these blinds; no huge lower lip; and scarcely any tongue. Two external spout-holes to the Right; one only to the Sperm. Look your last while they lie together: one will soon sink unrecorded. The Sperm’s broad brow wears prairie-like placidity, speculative indifference as to death; the Right’s amazing lip, pressed to the vessel’s side, speaks of enormous practical resolution in facing death. This Right Whale I take to have been a Stoic; the Sperm Whale, a Platonian, who might have taken up Spinoza in his latter years.

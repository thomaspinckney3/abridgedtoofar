ETYMOLOGY

(Supplied by a Late Consumptive Usher to a Grammar School.)

The pale Usher—threadbare in coat, heart, body, and brain; ever dusting his old lexicons with a queer handkerchief embellished with the gay flags of all the known nations. He loved to dust them; it mildly reminded him of his mortality.

"While you take in hand to teach what name a whale-fish is called, leaving out, through ignorance, the letter H, which almost alone maketh up the signification of the word, you deliver that which is not true." —*Hackluyt.*

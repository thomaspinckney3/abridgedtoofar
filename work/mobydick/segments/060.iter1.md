On Tower-hill a kedger holds up a painted board of the tragic scene in which he lost his leg—three whales, three boats; one boat, presumed to contain the missing leg in all its integrity, crunched by jaws. Ten years he has shown that picture and that stump to an incredulous world; his time of justification is come: his whales are as good as any published in Wapping; his stump unquestionable; but never a stump-speech—only ruefully contemplates his own amputation.

Throughout the Pacific and in Nantucket, New Bedford, and Sag Harbor, you will come across lively sketches graven by the fishermen themselves on Sperm Whale-teeth and ladies’ busks of Right Whale-bone—skrimmshander—wrought with the jack-knife alone.

Long exile from Christendom and civilization inevitably restores a man to that condition in which God placed him, _i.e._ what is called savagery. Your true whale-hunter is as much a savage as an Iroquois. I myself am a savage, owning no allegiance but to the King of the Cannibals; and ready at any moment to rebel against him. Now, one of the peculiar characteristics of the savage in his domestic hours, is his wonderful patience of industry. An ancient Hawaiian war-club or spear-paddle, in its full multiplicity and elaboration of carving, is as great a trophy of human perseverance as a Latin lexicon. As with the Hawaiian savage, so with the white sailor-savage. With the same marvellous patience, and with the same single shark’s tooth of his one poor jack-knife, he will carve you a bit of bone sculpture, not quite as workmanlike, but as close packed in its maziness of design, as the Greek savage, Achilles’s shield; and full of barbaric spirit and suggestiveness, as the prints of that fine old Dutch savage, Albert Durer.

Wooden whales from South Sea war-wood hang in forecastles; brass whales for knockers thump at country doors; sheet-iron whales weather-cock on spires.

In bony, ribby regions of earth, at the base of broken cliffs, you will discover Leviathan petrified in grass; in mountainous lands, along amphitheatrical ridges, the profiles of whales; but you must be a thorough whaleman, and mark your latitude and longitude, else you will lose your Soloma.

Nor when expandingly lifted by your subject, can you fail to trace out great whales in the starry heavens, with boats pursuing them; as when long filled with thoughts of war the Eastern nations saw armies locked in battle among the clouds. Thus at the North have I chased Leviathan round and round the Pole with the revolutions of the bright points that first defined him to me; and beneath the effulgent Antarctic skies I have boarded the Argo-Navis, and joined the chase against the starry Cetus far beyond the utmost stretch of Hydrus and the Flying Fish.

With a frigate’s anchors for bridle-bitts and fasces of harpoons for spurs, would I could mount that whale and leap the topmost skies!

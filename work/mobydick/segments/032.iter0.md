Ice and icebergs astern, the Pequod rolled into the bright Quito spring—the warmly cool, clear, ringing, perfumed, overflowing days; the starred and stately nights like haughty dames. But all that witchery turned inward, and more and more it wrought upon Ahab’s texture. Old age is wakeful; and Ahab, linked long with life, haunted the open air, muttering that to descend the scuttle to his grave-dug berth “feels like going down into one’s tomb.”

Nightly, when quietude fell, he would emerge, gripping the iron banister, often abstaining from patrolling the quarter-deck for his mates’ sleep’s sake—the crack of the ivory heel would have set dreams on the crunching teeth of sharks. But once the mood was on him too deep; he lumbered from taffrail to mainmast till Stubb came up, deprecatingly humorous, hinting at muffling the noise with a tow-stuffed globe for that ivory heel.

“Am I a cannon-ball, Stubb,” said Ahab, “that thou wouldst wad me that fashion? But go thy ways; I had forgot. Below to thy nightly grave… Down, dog, and kennel!”

Stubb started, then flared: he was not used to be spoken to so. “Avast!” gritted Ahab; “be called ten times a donkey, and a mule, and an ass, and begone, or I’ll clear the world of thee!” He advanced with such overbearing terrors that Stubb involuntarily retreated down the scuttle.

There he muttered, half for a blow, half for—what?—“down here on my knees and pray for him?” His eyes had flashed like powder-pans. Was he mad? A hot old man, with a conscience—“a kind of Tic-Dolly-row—worse nor a toothache.” Dough-Boy found his hammock sheets knotted, pillow hot as if a baked brick had lain there. What did he in the after hold nightly? Who had appointments with him? Queer, all queer; “but that’s against my principles. Think not, is my eleventh commandment; and sleep when you can, is my twelfth.” Yet even as he slung to hammock, he started: “Didn’t he call me a dog?—ten times a donkey—and pile jackasses on top of that!” His brow had flashed like a bleached bone. “By the Lord, I must have been dreaming—How? how? how?—the only way’s to stash it.” And in the morning, he would see how the plaguey juggling looked by daylight.

In behalf of the dignity of whaling, I would fain advance naught but substantiated facts. But after embattling his facts, an advocate who should wholly suppress a not unreasonable surmise, which might tell eloquently upon his cause—such an advocate, would he not be blameworthy?

There is a saltcellar of state, so called, and there may be a castor of state. How they use the salt, precisely—who knows? At coronation a king’s head is oiled—even as a head of salad. In truth, a mature man who uses hair-oil, unless medicinally, that man has probably got a quoggy spot in him somewhere.

Not olive, nor macassar, nor castor, nor bear’s, nor train, nor cod-liver. What then can it be but sperm oil in its unmanufactured, unpolluted state—the sweetest of all oils?

Think of that, ye loyal Britons! we whalemen supply your kings and queens with coronation stuff!

An advocate, after embattling his facts, may season them with a not unreasonable surmise.

Kings and queens, at coronation, are solemnly oiled—even as a head of salad. Can it be, though, that they anoint it with a view of making its interior run well, as they anoint machinery? In truth, a mature man who uses hair-oil, unless medicinally, that man has probably got a quoggy spot in him somewhere. As a general rule, he can’t amount to much in his totality. Not olive, nor macassar, nor castor, nor bear’s, nor train, nor cod-liver. What then can it be but sperm oil in its unmanufactured, unpolluted state—the sweetest of all oils?

Think of that, ye loyal Britons! we whalemen supply your kings and queens with coronation stuff!

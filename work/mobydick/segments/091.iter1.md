Though immense herds are sometimes met, small detached bands are often observed—schools—embracing from twenty to fifty. Two sorts: those almost entirely of females, and those mustering none but young vigorous males—forty-barrel-bulls.

In cavalier attendance on the females, you invariably see one full-grown male, not old; upon alarm, he gallantly falls in the rear and covers the flight of his ladies. A luxurious Ottoman, swimming with the solaces of a harem, he is of the largest proportions, while his concubines, even at full growth, are not more than a third his bulk—comparatively delicate, yet hereditarily entitled to en bon point. On their leisured rambles, they promenade the Equator, cheat summer in northern seas, then anticipate coolness in Oriental waters.

If any unwarrantably pert young Leviathan presume to draw close to a lady, with what prodigious fury the Bashaw assails and chases him! High times, indeed, if young rakes invade domestic bliss; though, do what the Bashaw will, he cannot keep the most notorious Lothario out of his bed; for, alas! all fish bed in common. But if the invader betake himself away at the first rush, then the lord insinuates his vast bulk among them again and revels there awhile—like pious Solomon devoutly worshipping among his thousand concubines. And, like certain other omnivorous roving lovers that might be named, my Lord Whale has no taste for the nursery, however much for the bower; and so, being a great traveller, he leaves his anonymous babies all over the world; every baby an exotic.

As youth declines and dumps increase; reflection lends her pauses; lassitude overtakes the sated Turk: love of ease and virtue supplants love for maidens; he forswears, disbands the harem, and, grown a sulky exemplary soul, goes alone saying his prayers, warning each young Leviathan from amorous errors. Hence the harem’s lord is the schoolmaster—satirically so; after going to school, he preaches the folly of it. The title would seem derived from the school itself; yet some have surmised it came of the memoirs of Vidocq, and what sort of country-schoolmaster that famous Frenchman was in his younger days, and the occult lessons he inculcated.

The same secludedness marks all ancient Sperm Whales; almost universally a lone whale proves an aged one—like moss-bearded Daniel Boone, wifing Nature in the wilderness of waters.

The forty-barrel-bull schools are larger and a contrast: like a mob of collegians, full of fight, fun, and wickedness, tumbling round the world at a rollicking rate; proverbially the most dangerous to encounter, save the wondrous grizzled whales that fight like fiends. Struck, a bull is deserted by all his comrades; but strike a member of the harem, and her companions swim around her with every token of concern, lingering so near and so long as themselves to fall a prey.

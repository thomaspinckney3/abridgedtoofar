Next morning Stubb accosted Flask.

"Such a queer dream, King-Post, I never had. You know the old man's ivory leg; I dreamed he kicked me with it; when I tried to kick back, I kicked my leg right off! Then, presto! Ahab seemed a pyramid, and I, like a blazing fool, kept kicking at it. But all the while I was thinking, it was not much of an insult—only a false leg, only a whalebone cane; only a playful cudgelling. The foot part—what a small sort of end; this insult is whittled down to a point only.

"But now comes the greatest joke. While I was battering at the pyramid, a badger-haired old merman with a hump on his back takes me by the shoulders and slews me round. 'What are you 'bout?' says he. Such a phiz! 'And what business is that of yours, Mr. Humpback? Do you want a kick?' By the lord, Flask, no sooner said than he turned his stern to me—and his stern was stuck full of marlinspikes, points out. Says I, 'I guess I won't kick you.' 'Wise Stubb,' said he, 'wise Stubb, wise Stubb.' So I thought I might as well kick the pyramid again. But he roared, 'Stop that kicking! Let's argue the insult. Captain Ahab kicked ye with his ivory leg, didn't he? Wise Stubb, what have you to complain of? You were kicked by a great man, with a beautiful ivory leg. It's an honor. In old England the greatest lords think it great glory to be slapped by a queen, and made garter-knights of; but, be _your_ boast, Stubb, that ye were kicked by old Ahab, and made a wise man of. Account his kicks honors; _be_ kicked by him; on no account kick back; for you can't help yourself.' With that, he swam off into the air. I rolled over, and there I was in my hammock! What do you think of that, Flask?"

"I don't know; it seems a sort of foolish to me, tho.'"

"May be; may be. But it's made a wise man of me, Flask. Best let the old man alone; never speak to him, whatever he says. Halloa! What's that he shouts? Hark!"

"Mast-head, there! Look sharp, all of ye! There are whales hereabouts!

"If ye see a white one, split your lungs for him!

"A white whale—did ye mark that, man? There's something special in the wind, Flask. Ahab has that that's bloody on his mind. But, mum; he comes this way."

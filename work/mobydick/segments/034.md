Next morning Stubb accosted Flask.

"Such a queer dream, King-Post, I never had. You know the old man's ivory leg? I dreamed he kicked me with it; and when I tried to kick back, I kicked my leg right off! Then, presto! Ahab seemed a pyramid, and I, like a blazing fool, kept kicking at it. But all the while I thought it not much of an insult — 'not a real leg, only a false leg. There's a mighty difference between a living thump and a dead thump. What's his leg but a cane — a whalebone cane. Only a playful cudgelling — only a whaleboning.' But now comes the greatest joke. While I was battering at the pyramid, a sort of badger-haired old merman, with a hump on his back, takes me by the shoulders and slews me round. 'What are you 'bout?' says he. Such a phiz! 'What am I about?' says I. 'What business is that of yours, Mr. Humpback? Do _you_ want a kick?' Lord, Flask, no sooner had I said that, than he turned his stern to me, dragged up a clout of seaweed — and his stern was stuck full of marlinspikes, points out. 'I guess I won't kick you, old fellow.' 'Wise Stubb,' said he, 'wise Stubb;' muttering it like a chimney hag. Then: 'let's argue the insult. Captain Ahab kicked ye with his ivory leg, didn't he? Well then, wise Stubb, what have you to complain of? Ye were kicked by a great man, and with a beautiful ivory leg. It's an honor. In old England lords think it great glory to be slapped by a queen, and made garter-knights of; but be _your_ boast, Stubb, that ye were kicked by old Ahab, and made a wise man of. Account his kicks honors; and on no account kick back; for you can't help yourself, wise Stubb.' With that, he swam off into the air. I snored, and there I was in my hammock. What do you think of that dream, Flask?"

"I don't know; it seems a sort of foolish to me, tho.'"

"May be; but it's made a wise man of me. The best thing you can do is to let the old man alone; never speak to him, whatever he says. Halloa! What's that he shouts? Hark!"

"Mast-head, there! Look sharp, all of ye! There are whales hereabouts!

"If ye see a white one, split your lungs for him!"

"What do you think of that now, Flask? A white whale — did ye mark that, man? There's something special in the wind. Ahab has that that's bloody on his mind. But, mum; he comes this way."

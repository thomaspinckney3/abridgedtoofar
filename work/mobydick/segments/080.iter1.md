Now comes the Baling of the Case. Regard the Sperm Whale’s head as a solid oblong; sideways divide it into two quoins (Quoin is not a Euclidean term; it belongs to the pure nautical mathematics. I know not that it has been defined before): the lower, bone—cranium and jaws; the upper, an unctuous mass, wholly free of bone, its broad forward end forming the apparent vertical forehead. Horizontally subdivide that upper quoin at mid-forehead: the lower subpart is the junk—an immense honeycomb of oil, ten thousand infiltrated cells crossing and recrossing with tough elastic white fibres; the upper subpart is the Case—“the great Heidelburgh Tun of the Sperm Whale.” As that famous tierce is mystically carved, so the whale’s vast plaited forehead makes emblematical devices upon his tun; and as Heidelburgh brimmed with the wines of the Rhenish valleys, so this tun holds the most precious of his oily vintages—spermaceti in its absolutely pure, limpid, and odoriferous state. In life perfectly fluid, after death it concretes, sending forth delicate crystalline shoots like the first thin ice. A large Case yields about five hundred gallons; much is spilled and lost in the ticklish business of securing what you can.

I know not with what fine and costly material the Heidelburgh Tun was coated within, but in superlative richness that coating could not possibly have compared with the silken pearl-coloured membrane, like the lining of a fine pelisse, forming the inner surface of the Sperm Whale’s case. The Case embraces the entire length of the head’s top; if a whale be eighty feet, then more than twenty-six feet is the tun’s depth when hoisted lengthwise against a ship’s side. In decapitating, the cutting-tool is brought close to where entrance is afterwards forced into the spermaceti magazine; heed is needful, lest a careless, untimely stroke invade the sanctuary and wastingly let out the treasure. It is this decapitated end that is at last elevated out of water and retained there by the enormous cutting tackles—a wilderness of ropes in that quarter.

Thus much being said, attend now, I pray you, to that marvellous and—in this particular instance—almost fatal operation whereby the Sperm Whale’s great Heidelburgh Tun is tapped.

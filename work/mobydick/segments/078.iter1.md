Cross the deck to the Right Whale’s head. If the Sperm’s front is a rounded Roman chariot, the Right’s bears an inelegant resemblance to a gigantic galliot-toed shoe; old Dutch voyagers likened it to a shoemaker’s last—ample enough to lodge that nursery old woman and all her brood. According to your point of view, the head shifts aspect: stand atop; the two F-shaped spoutholes make it an enormous bass-viol; fix on the green barnacled “crown” or “bonnet,” with live crabs nesting, and it is a diademed king of the sea—though a sulky one. Look at that hanging lower lip—what a huge sulk and pout! twenty feet long, five deep; a sulk to yield some five hundred gallons of oil.

A great pity, now, that this unfortunate whale should be hare-lipped. The fissure is about a foot across. Probably the mother during an important interval was sailing down the Peruvian coast, when earthquakes caused the beach to gape. Over this lip, as over a slippery threshold, we now slide into the mouth. Upon my word were I at Mackinaw, I should take this to be the inside of an Indian wigwam. “Good Lord! is this the road that Jonah went?” The roof is about twelve feet high, and runs to a pretty sharp angle, as if there were a regular ridge-pole there; while these ribbed, arched, hairy sides present those wondrous, half-vertical, scimetar-shaped slats—say three hundred on a side—depending from the crown-bone: Venetian blinds whose hairy fringes strain the brit-seas. Marks and hollows in the central blinds serve some to count age, as oaks by rings. Old travelers called them whiskers, hogs’ bristles, fins; and, indeed, the ladies’ busks draw their stiffening from the same bone. It was in Queen Anne’s time that the bone was in its glory, the farthingale being then all the fashion; and, even so, in a shower, with the like thoughtlessness, do we nowadays fly under the same jaws for protection; the umbrella being a tent spread over the same bone.

Forget blinds and whiskers; stand within among colonnades of bone and fancy the great Haarlem organ; for carpet, a soft Turkey rug—the tongue—fat and tender, a six-barreler by the look.

To sum up: no great well of sperm here; no ivory teeth; no long slender mandible; while in the Sperm there are none of these blinds; no huge lower lip; and scarcely any tongue. Two external spout-holes to the Right; one only to the Sperm. Look your last while they lie together: one will soon sink unrecorded. The Sperm’s broad brow wears prairie-like placidity, speculative indifference as to death; the Right’s amazing lip, pressed to the vessel’s side, speaks of enormous practical resolution in facing death. This Right Whale I take to have been a Stoic; the Sperm Whale, a Platonian, who might have taken up Spinoza in his latter years.

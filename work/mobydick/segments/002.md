EXTRACTS

This burrower of a Sub-Sub has gone through the long Vaticans and street-stalls of the earth, picking up whatever random allusions to whales he could find. You must not take these higgledy-piggledy statements for gospel cetology; they are a glancing bird's eye view of what has been said and sung of Leviathan.

So fare thee well, poor devil of a Sub-Sub, whose commentator I am. Thou belongest to that hopeless, sallow tribe which no wine of this world will ever warm. Give it up, Sub-Subs! For by how much the more pains ye take to please the world, by so much the more shall ye go thankless! But gulp down your tears and hie aloft to the royal-mast with your hearts; for your friends are clearing out the seven-storied heavens against your coming. Here ye strike but splintered hearts together—there, ye shall strike unsplinterable glasses!

Thus we lay, chatting and napping by turns; Queequeg now and then throwing his brown tattooed legs over mine and drawing them back—so sociable were we—till, by our confabulations, what little nappishness remained departed. Wearied of our recumbent posture, we found ourselves sitting up, the clothes tucked well about us, leaning against the head-board; four knees drawn up close, two noses bending over them as if kneepans were warming-pans. It was chilly; no fire; yet we felt nice and snug. Truly to enjoy bodily warmth, some small part of you must be cold; “there is no quality in this world that is not what it is merely by contrast.” Nothing exists in itself. If you flatter yourself that you are all over comfortable, and have been so a long time, then you cannot be said to be comfortable any more. For this reason a sleeping apartment should never be furnished with a fire, which is one of the luxurious discomforts of the rich. For the height of deliciousness, have nothing but the blanket between you and the cold; then you lie like the one warm spark in the heart of an arctic crystal.

I keep my eyes shut between sheets, to concentrate snugness; “Because no man can ever feel his own identity aright except his eyes be closed; as if darkness were indeed the proper element of our essences, though light be more congenial to our clayey part;” but opening them to the coarse outer gloom of midnight, I felt a revulsion, and did not object to Queequeg’s hint to strike a light; he desired a few quiet puffs from his Tomahawk. —yet see how elastic our stiff prejudices grow when love once comes to bend them. For now I liked nothing better than his smoking by me, for he seemed full of serene household joy. With our shaggy jackets drawn about our shoulders, we passed the Tomahawk to and fro, till a blue hanging tester of smoke slowly grew over us, illuminated by the flame of the new-lit lamp. Whether that undulating canopy rolled him to far scenes I know not, but he now spoke of his native island; eager to hear, I begged him to go on; he gladly complied.

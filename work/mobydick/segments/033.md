When Stubb had departed, Ahab called for his ivory stool and pipe; lighting it at the binnacle, he sat and smoked. In old Norse times the thrones of the Danish kings were of narwhale tusk; and a Khan of the plank, a king of the sea, a great lord of Leviathans was Ahab.

The thick vapor came in quick and constant puffs, blowing back into his face. "How now," he soliloquized, "this smoking no longer soothes. Oh, my pipe! hard must it go with me if thy charm be gone! What business have I with this thing meant for sereneness—mild white vapors among mild white hairs, not among torn iron-grey locks like mine. I'll smoke no more—"

He tossed the lighted pipe into the sea. The fire hissed; the ship shot by the bubble the sinking pipe made. With slouched hat, Ahab lurchingly paced the planks.

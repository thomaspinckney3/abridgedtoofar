Had you stepped aboard the Pequod at a certain juncture of this post-mortemizing, and strolled forward nigh the windlass, you had scanned, lying lengthwise in the lee scuppers, a strange, enigmatical object. Not the wondrous cistern in the whale’s huge head; not the prodigy of his unhinged lower jaw; not the miracle of his symmetrical tail; none of these would so surprise you, as half a glimpse of that unaccountable cone,—longer than a Kentuckian is tall, nigh a foot in diameter at the base, and jet-black as Yojo, the ebony idol of Queequeg. And an idol, indeed, it is; or, rather, in old times, its likeness was. Such an idol as that found in the secret groves of Queen Maachah in Judea; and for worshipping which, King Asa, her son, did depose her, and destroyed the idol, and burnt it for an abomination at the brook Kedron, as darkly set forth in the 15th chapter of the First Book of Kings.

Look at the sailor called the mincer, who, with two allies, backs the grandissimus, as the mariners call it, and staggers off with it; laid upon the forecastle, he cylindrically removes its dark pelt, as an African hunter the pelt of a boa; then turns it inside out like a pantaloon-leg, gives it a stretching till near doubled, and hangs it in the rigging to dry. Taken down, he lops a length, cuts two slits for arm-holes at the broad end, and lengthwise slips himself bodily into it. The mincer now stands invested in the full canonicals of his calling. Immemorial to all his order, this investiture alone will adequately protect him, while employed in the peculiar functions of his office.

That office consists in mincing the horse-pieces of blubber for the pots; performed at a wooden horse against the bulwarks, with a tub beneath, into which the slices drop, fast as the sheets from a rapt orator’s desk. Arrayed in decent black; occupying a conspicuous pulpit; intent on bible leaves—what a candidate for an archbishopric; what a lad for a Pope, were this mincer!

“Bible leaves! Bible leaves!”—so cry the mates, bidding him cut as thin as may be, that boiling may be sped, the yield increased, and, it may be, the quality refined.

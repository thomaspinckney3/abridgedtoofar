## Cetology

Already we are boldly launched upon the deep; but before the Pequod's weedy hull rolls alongside the barnacled hulls of the leviathan, it is well to attend to some systematic exhibition of the whale in his broad genera. It is no easy task. The classification of the constituents of a chaos — nothing less is here essayed. "No branch of Zoology is so much involved as Cetology," wrote Captain Scoresby; and Cuvier, Hunter, Beale, and the rest speak of impenetrable veils and fields strewn with thorns. Of real knowledge there is little; of books, a plenty. Only a few of the authors — Scoresby among them — ever saw a living whale, and Scoresby knew nothing of the sperm whale, compared with which the Greenland whale is an usurper upon the throne of the seas. Hear ye, good people all: the Greenland whale is deposed; the great sperm whale now reigneth!

I promise nothing complete, because any human thing supposed to be complete must for that very reason infallibly be faulty. I am the architect, not the builder. Some preliminaries: waiving Linnæus's edict, I take the good old-fashioned ground that the whale is a fish, and call upon holy Jonah to back me. He differs from other fish in having lungs and warm blood. Defined by externals: *a whale is a spouting fish with a horizontal tail.* There you have him.

Attempts to classify by baleen, hump, fin, or teeth all founder, for these features are dispersed irregularly among the kinds. On this rock every whale-naturalist has split. Nothing remains but to take hold of the whales bodily, in their entire liberal volume, and sort them that way. Hence, a Bibliographical system. According to magnitude I divide the whales into three primary BOOKS (subdivisible into CHAPTERS): **I. THE FOLIO WHALE; II. THE OCTAVO WHALE; III. THE DUODECIMO WHALE.**

The **Folios** comprise the largest kinds. As type I present the Sperm Whale — the largest inhabitant of the globe, most formidable, most majestic, most valuable in commerce, and the only source of spermaceti. Under this Book also fall the Right Whale (venerable, first hunted, yielder of baleen and inferior oil); the Fin-Back, that solitary misanthrope whose vertical fin stands like a gnomon upon the sea, the banished Cain of his race; the Hump Back, most gamesome and light-hearted; the Razor Back, of whom little is known but his name; and the Sulphur Bottom, with his brimstone belly, who would run off with rope-walks of line. Adieu, Sulphur Bottom!

The **Octavoes** — middling whales — include the Grampus, whose blowing has furnished a proverb; the Black Fish, better called the Hyena Whale for his everlasting Mephistophelean grin; the Narwhale, or Nostril Whale, whose sinister tusk some have thought a poison antidote, others an ice-piercer, and which would certainly serve him well as a folder in reading pamphlets; the Killer, savage as a Feejee — though we are all killers, Bonapartes and sharks included; and the Thrasher, who flogs the Folio whale's back as some schoolmasters flog their way through the world.

The **Duodecimoes** are the small porpoises: the Huzza Porpoise, who swims in hilarious shoals like caps in a Fourth-of-July crowd (and is the Sperm Whale himself in miniature); the Algerine Porpoise, a pirate of the Pacific; and the Mealy-mouthed Porpoise, gentleman-like but for the white lower face that makes him look as if caught in a felonious visit to a meal-bag.

Beyond the Duodecimo this system does not proceed. There remain a rabble of half-fabulous whales — Bottle-Nose, Junk, Pudding-Headed, Iceberg, Blue, and the rest — mere sounds, full of Leviathanism, but signifying nothing.

I leave my cetological system standing thus unfinished, even as the great Cathedral of Cologne was left, with the crane still standing upon the uncompleted tower. For small erections may be finished by their first architects; grand ones, true ones, ever leave the copestone to posterity. God keep me from ever completing anything. This whole book is but a draught — nay, but the draught of a draught. Oh, Time, Strength, Cash, and Patience!

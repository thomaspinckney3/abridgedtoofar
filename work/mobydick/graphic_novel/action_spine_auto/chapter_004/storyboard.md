# Moby-Dick Ch. 4: The Counterpane — Action Spine

Source chapter: Chapter 4. The Counterpane

Adaptation principle: Track Ishmael’s waking discovery, his choice to observe Queequeg, the roommate’s courteous offer, the odd toilette, the prompted modesty, and the harpoon shave to the exit; fold digressions and metaphors into captions without staging them.

## Action-Oriented Synopsis

I wake at dawn to find Queequeg's tattooed arm thrown over me like a spouse. I try to free myself and rouse him, only to discover his tomahawk in the bed. I finally wake him and, with my fears gone, I lie still to watch this curious bedfellow. He politely offers by signs to dress first and give me the room, which I accept while I stare. He dresses in outlandish order—hat on, boots pulled on under the bed—then hobbles about pinched by them. Seeing the open window and his indecorous near-nakedness, I urge him to get into his pantaloons, and he complies. He washes only his torso, shaves with his harpoon, then finishes and marches out with jacket and weapon like a marshal’s baton.

## Caption Spine

1. Upon waking about daylight, I found Queequeg’s tattooed arm thrown over me, blending with the patchwork counterpane until its weight told me I was hugged.
2. I tried to unlock his bridegroom clasp and wriggle free, when a slight scratch and a fling of the quilt showed the tomahawk sleeping by his side.
3. By dint of much wriggling and expostulation I roused him; he shook himself like a Newfoundland dog and sat up, while I lay quietly eyeing him with no serious misgivings, bent on narrowly observing so curious a creature.
4. He jumped out and, by signs and sounds, gave me to understand he would dress first and then leave me to dress, which I took for a very civilized overture and let him proceed.
5. I saw him commence at top with a very tall beaver hat, then crush himself—boots in hand and hat on—under the bed, and at last emerge dented, creaking and limping about the room.
6. Seeing the window bare and his indecorous figure, I begged him to accelerate his toilet and get into his pantaloons, which he did.
7. To my amazement he washed only chest, arms, and hands, then lathered his face and, unsheathing the harpoon head, fell to harpooning his cheeks before marching out wrapped in his pilot monkey jacket, sporting the weapon like a marshal’s baton.

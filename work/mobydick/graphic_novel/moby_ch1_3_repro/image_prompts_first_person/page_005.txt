Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- visual period: 1840s American seaports: Manhattan Battery to New Bedford whaling town, interiors of a rough waterfront inn
- line and color: Clean ink line with etched, woodcut texture; limited winter palette (slate blues, tar blacks, whale-bone ivories, candle ambers); occasional spectral whites for the ‘phantom whale’ overlays
- lighting: Cold exterior daylight and dusk; smoky lamplight interiors with chiaroscuro; strong candle pools at night; sudden lamp flare for crisis beats
- recurring motifs: Reflections and ripples of water overlaying scenes; Ghost-silhouette of a hooded white whale in negative space; Whale jawbone arch framing barroom moments; Frosted breath in winter streets; Lamp and candle halos that punctuate tension

Character continuity:
- Ishmael: Young, weather-worn New Englander; keen eyes; old carpet-bag in hand Costume: Dark pea coat, woolen scarf, knit cap; fingerless gloves; later, shirt-sleeves in the room Continuity: Carries the carpet-bag from packing through New Bedford arrival; VO captions in first person throughout
- Landlord Peter Coffin (aka Jonah at bar): Lean, grinning innkeeper behind a bar set in a whale’s jawbone Costume: Rolled shirt-sleeves, tarry apron, short waistcoat; later a rough coat when carrying a lamp Continuity: Always near the bar downstairs; later appears upstairs with a lamp; occasionally whittles a toothpick
- Queequeg: Powerful, tattooed South Sea islander; scalp-knot; calm, methodical manner Costume: Shaggy poncho; bare tattooed chest/arms when indoors; carries tomahawk-pipe; small idol and a preserved head as curio Continuity: Idol set briefly at fireplace for rite, then bagged; tomahawk shown as pipe and then stashed before sleep
- Jonah (bartender identity of Coffin for service beats): Same as Peter Coffin, specifically framed beneath the jawbone arch pouring drinks Costume: Bar apron, sleeves rolled, brimmers in hand Continuity: Use ‘Jonah at service’ vibe during Grampus crew storm-in moments
- Bulkington: Tall, broad-shouldered, reserved sailor, coat crusted with ice Costume: Long dark coat, sea boots; hat low over brow Continuity: Only glimpsed in the doorway departing amid revelry

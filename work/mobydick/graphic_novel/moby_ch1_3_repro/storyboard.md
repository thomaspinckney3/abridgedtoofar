## Scope

Ch. 1–3 excerpt: Ishmael’s opening reflections through his first night at the Spouter-Inn with Queequeg

## Style Bible

- **Visual Period:** 1840s American seaports: Manhattan Battery to New Bedford whaling town, interiors of a rough waterfront inn
- **Line And Color:** Clean ink line with etched, woodcut texture; limited winter palette (slate blues, tar blacks, whale-bone ivories, candle ambers); occasional spectral whites for the ‘phantom whale’ overlays
- **Lighting:** Cold exterior daylight and dusk; smoky lamplight interiors with chiaroscuro; strong candle pools at night; sudden lamp flare for crisis beats
- **Lettering:** Ishmael VO in a restrained literary voice—small caps italics; dialogue in rough serif with modest dialect; SFX hand-lettered, restrained
- **Recurring Motifs:** Reflections and ripples of water overlaying scenes; Ghost-silhouette of a hooded white whale in negative space; Whale jawbone arch framing barroom moments; Frosted breath in winter streets; Lamp and candle halos that punctuate tension

## Characters

- **Ishmael:** visual anchor: Young, weather-worn New Englander; keen eyes; old carpet-bag in hand; costume: Dark pea coat, woolen scarf, knit cap; fingerless gloves; later, shirt-sleeves in the room; expression range: Wry, observant, determined; shifts from wary to accepting; continuity note: Carries the carpet-bag from packing through New Bedford arrival; VO captions in first person throughout
- **Landlord Peter Coffin (aka Jonah at bar):** visual anchor: Lean, grinning innkeeper behind a bar set in a whale’s jawbone; costume: Rolled shirt-sleeves, tarry apron, short waistcoat; later a rough coat when carrying a lamp; expression range: Genial, sly, practical; quick to mediate; continuity note: Always near the bar downstairs; later appears upstairs with a lamp; occasionally whittles a toothpick
- **Queequeg:** visual anchor: Powerful, tattooed South Sea islander; scalp-knot; calm, methodical manner; costume: Shaggy poncho; bare tattooed chest/arms when indoors; carries tomahawk-pipe; small idol and a preserved head as curio; expression range: Composed, courteous; momentary startle at intrusion; then warm and inviting; continuity note: Idol set briefly at fireplace for rite, then bagged; tomahawk shown as pipe and then stashed before sleep
- **Jonah (bartender identity of Coffin for service beats):** visual anchor: Same as Peter Coffin, specifically framed beneath the jawbone arch pouring drinks; costume: Bar apron, sleeves rolled, brimmers in hand; expression range: Brisk, service-smile, unflappable; continuity note: Use ‘Jonah at service’ vibe during Grampus crew storm-in moments
- **Bulkington:** visual anchor: Tall, broad-shouldered, reserved sailor, coat crusted with ice; costume: Long dark coat, sea boots; hat low over brow; expression range: Quiet, distant, steadfast; continuity note: Only glimpsed in the doorway departing amid revelry

# Moby-Dick — Chapter 2: The Carpet-Bag (Action Spine)

Source chapter: Moby-Dick; or, The Whale — Chapter 2. The Carpet-Bag

Adaptation principle: Focus on concrete turns: arrival and delay, frugal lodging decision, rejecting costly inns, mistaken entry into a church, and choosing the Spouter Inn; fold historical pride, sermons, and wind digressions into captions or omit as non-driving rhetoric.

## Action-Oriented Synopsis

Ishmael packs his carpet-bag, reaches New Bedford on a Saturday night, and discovers the Nantucket packet has already sailed so he must wait until Monday. With little money and no acquaintances, he decides to find inexpensive lodgings and not be too particular. He rejects the lively, costly inns he passes and heads toward the waterfront in search of cheaper quarters. Mistaking a public-looking building for an inn, he stumbles into a church service and immediately backs out. Near the docks he finds the Spouter Inn, deems it a cheap, suitable place despite its ominous name, and goes to see what sort of place it may be.

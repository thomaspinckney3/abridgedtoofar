# Moby-Dick, Ch. 2 — The Carpet-Bag (Action Spine)

Source chapter: Chapter 2. The Carpet-Bag

Adaptation principle: Prioritize Ishmael’s concrete travel, lodging hunt, and choices; fold rhetoric and digressions into captions without staging hypotheticals.

## Character Continuity

- Ishmael (narrator-traveler): Young adult seaman, lean and travel-worn; dark wool coat, rough scarf, simple trousers, scuffed patched boots; carries a small old brown carpet-bag under one arm; wind-reddened face.
- Black Preacher (preacher at the church Ishmael enters by mistake): Middle-aged Black man in a plain dark coat at a simple wooden pulpit, one hand striking or holding a large dark book; stern, fervent posture.
- Black Congregation (parishioners in the church): Rows of Black men and women in plain winter clothing and caps, seated close together in a dim, smoky-lit meeting room, turning to look toward the doorway.

## Prop Continuity

- carpet_bag: Small, old, brown fabric-and-leather carpet-bag with worn handles and scuffs. Avoid confusion: Do not depict as a hard suitcase or a seaman’s chest.
- patched_boots: Well-worn leather boots with visible sole wear and patches at the toes. Avoid confusion: Do not render as new or fashionable boots.
- inn_sign_spout: Weathered wooden swinging sign with a painted white jet of spray; hangs by iron brackets and creaks in the wind. Avoid confusion: No readable text; do not replace the spray with a whale image.
- ash_box: Low wooden box half-full of grey ash in a dim porch; loose ash dust around it. Avoid confusion: Do not depict as a coal scuttle or metal trash can.
- pulpit_and_book: Plain wooden pulpit; large dark book with no readable text on the cover or pages. Avoid confusion: Do not adorn with ornate church symbols or readable scripture.

## Action-Oriented Synopsis

I pack my carpet-bag, leave Manhattan for New Bedford, and find the Nantucket packet has sailed so I must wait till Monday. I resolve to sail only in a Nantucket craft, not from New Bedford. With little money and no acquaintances, I decide to find cheap lodgings for the two nights and to ask the price without being too particular. I tramp the freezing streets, pass the bright, costly inns, and head waterward for the cheapest houses. I enter a low public building, stumble over an ash-box, discover a negro church in full sermon, and back out. I come upon the Spouter Inn’s creaking sign, judge it cheap despite the ominous name, and prepare to go in.

## Caption Spine

1. I stuffed a shirt or two into my old carpet-bag, quit old Manhatto, reached New Bedford on a Saturday night in December, and learned the Nantucket packet had sailed—no reaching there till Monday.
2. My mind was made up to sail in no other than a Nantucket craft, so I would not ship from New Bedford.
3. With only a few pieces of silver and knowing no one, I must eat and sleep somewhere; so I told myself—be sure to inquire the price, and don’t be too particular.
4. I paced the dreary, icy streets, passed the jolly, expensive inns, and by instinct followed the ways waterward for the cheapest lodgings.
5. Entering a low, smoky-lighted building, I stumbled over an ash-box, saw a hundred black faces and a black preacher beating a book, and I backed out of ‘The Trap.’
6. At last I saw the swinging sign of the Spouter Inn—Peter Coffin—ominous, yet the dim, dilapidated house promised cheap lodgings; I scraped the ice from my feet to go in and see.

## Omitted Material

- **Extended praise of Nantucket’s whaling primacy and historical anecdotes (Tyre and Carthage, Red-Men, cobblestones).:** Rhetorical background; it supports his decision to prefer a Nantucket craft, which is kept as a single decision frame.
- **Long digression on Euroclydon, Lazarus and Dives, and contrasting indoor comfort with outdoor misery.:** Philosophical comparison; no change to the physical action beyond reinforcing the cold, which is conveyed visually and in captions.
- **Gomorrah and Tophet jokes and hyperbole about the ash-box and church.:** Figurative color; the real action is Ishmael’s mistaken entry and exit, which is depicted directly.

## Visual Safety Notes

- Do not render any readable text on signs, books, or labels.
- Treat the church scene with respect: avoid caricature; portray the preacher and congregation plainly and realistically.
- Do not literalize metaphors (Tophet, Angel of Doom, Gomorrah, Lazarus, Dives); keep all supernatural or allegorical elements out of the imagery.
- Avoid inserting ships, whales, or Nantucket identifiers not physically present in the scene.

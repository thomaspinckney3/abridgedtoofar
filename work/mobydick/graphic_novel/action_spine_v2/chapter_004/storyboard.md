## Action-Oriented Synopsis

I wake at daylight to find Queequeg’s tattooed arm thrown over me, confusing it with the patchwork counterpane until its weight tells me he is hugging me. I wriggle and call to free myself from his bridegroom clasp, roll over, and discover his tomahawk beside us, which makes me urge him harder to wake. Queequeg finally stirs, pulls back his arm, shakes himself, and sits up staring while I quietly watch him without misgivings. By signs he offers to dress first and then leave me the room, and I accept while I keep observing his ways. He begins his odd toilet by putting on his tall beaver hat and booting himself under the bed, then limps about until at my urging he hastily gets into his pantaloons. He lathers and coolly shaves before the mirror with the steel head of his harpoon, which I marvel at. Finishing, he wraps himself in his pilot monkey jacket and marches out, sporting his harpoon like a marshal’s baton.

## Caption Spine

1. At daylight I found Queequeg’s tattooed arm thrown over me, so blended with the patchwork counterpane that only its weight told me he was hugging me.
2. I tried to unlock his bridegroom clasp, wriggling and calling, till rolling over I felt a scratch and, throwing aside the counterpane, found his tomahawk sleeping by his side.
3. At length I extracted a grunt; he drew back his arm, shook himself like a Newfoundland just from the water, and sat up staring, while I lay quietly eyeing him.
4. By signs he gave me to understand he would dress first and then leave me to dress after, a very civilized overture that I accepted while I watched him.
5. He commenced at top with a very tall beaver hat, then crushed himself under the bed to boot himself, and I begged him to accelerate his toilet and particularly to get into his pantaloons.
6. To my amazement he dipped hard soap, took the harpoon, slipped off the stock, whetted the head on his boot, and began a vigorous harpooning of his cheeks before the scrap of mirror.
7. The rest of his toilet being soon achieved, I watched him proudly march out wrapped in his great pilot monkey jacket, sporting his harpoon like a marshal’s baton.

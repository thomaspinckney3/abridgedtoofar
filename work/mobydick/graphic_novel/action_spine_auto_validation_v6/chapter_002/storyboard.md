# Moby-Dick Ch. 2 — The Carpet-Bag: Action Spine

Source chapter: Chapter 2. The Carpet-Bag

Adaptation principle: Reduce the chapter to the concrete decisions and path-changes: delayed passage, frugal search for lodging, mistaken church entry and retreat, and resolving to try the Spouter Inn.

## Action-Oriented Synopsis

Ishmael reaches New Bedford on a bitter Saturday night and discovers the Nantucket packet has sailed, delaying him until Monday. Needing food and a bed, he chooses frugality, searches the dark streets, and rejects the bright, expensive inns while heading waterward. Mistaking a smoky doorway for an inn, he blunders into a church service and quickly backs out. He finds the Spouter Inn, deems its ominous sign cheap enough, and resolves to go in.

## Caption Spine

1. I arrived in New Bedford on a Saturday night in December, only to learn the Nantucket packet had already sailed, and I must wait till Monday.
2. Sounding my pocket and finding only a few pieces of silver, I paced the dreary streets, shunned the jolly, costly houses, and steered waterward for the cheapest lodgings.
3. Taking a smoky light for a public house, I stumbled in, opened an inner door upon a negro church, and muttering, backed out from that wretched entertainment.
4. At last I spied the swinging sign of the Spouter Inn—Peter Coffin—ominous names; yet here, thought I, is the very spot for cheap lodgings—let us scrape the ice from our frosted feet and see what sort of a place this may be.

# Moby-Dick — Chapter 1: Loomings (Action Spine, Frames 3–8)

Source chapter: Chapter 1. Loomings

Adaptation principle: Reduce Ishmael’s reflective opening to concrete decision beats: I decide to go to sea, I choose the sailor’s role, I resolve on whaling, and I welcome the voyage—folding the chapter’s catalogues and analogies into captions over grounded, non-hypothetical postures.

## Action-Oriented Synopsis

Feeling my hypos rise and growing grim, I decide to get to sea at once. I choose to go as a simple sailor rather than as a passenger, officer, or cook. In this instance I make up my mind to ship on a whaling voyage. I welcome the whaling voyage, drawn by the great whale and my itch for things remote.

## Caption Spine

1. Whenever it is a damp, drizzly November in my soul, I account it high time to get to sea.
2. No, when I go to sea, I go as a simple sailor, right before the mast, not as passenger, Commodore, or cook; being paid and breathing the fore-castle air suit me best.
3. But wherefore it was that I should now take it into my head to go on a whaling voyage, I cannot fully tell—only that I now resolved to ship for one.
4. By reason of these things, then, the whaling voyage was welcome—the great flood-gates of the wonder-world swung open to me, with the whale midmost.

## Omitted Material

- **Extended panorama of Manhattoes, Battery crowds, and miles of water-gazers.:** Descriptive tableau that reinforces a mood and general human attraction to water without changing Ishmael’s situation; folded into decision frames per selection rules.
- **Country paths to water, metaphysical professor in the desert, artist of the Saco valley, Prairies without water, Niagara as sand, poet deliberating on Rockaway.:** Rhetorical analogies and examples that support the theme but do not advance the chapter’s concrete action; omitted to keep the spine tight.
- **Catalog of offices (Commodore, Captain, Cook) with humorous asides on broiled fowls and Egyptian mummies.:** Comic digressions used as reasons; their functional meaning (preference for being a common sailor) is folded into a single decision frame.
- **Hypothetical order to sweep decks and the ‘scales of the New Testament,’ universal thump discourse.:** Illustrative hypotheticals and philosophical reframing; acceptance is integrated into the sailor-role choice without depicting non-occurring actions.
- **Fates as stage managers, grand programme bill, contested election and battle in Afghanistan.:** Figurative attribution and worldly billposting do not constitute present action; their import is summarized in the whaling-resolution frame.
- **Persians and Greeks revering the sea, Narcissus and the ungraspable phantom of life.:** Mythic-philosophic support for ocean fascination; omitted to preserve forward movement from decision to resolve.

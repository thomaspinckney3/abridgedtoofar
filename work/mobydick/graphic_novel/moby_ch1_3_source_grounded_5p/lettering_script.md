# Moby-Dick — Loomings to The Spouter-Inn: Ishmael Meets Queequeg Lettering Script

Captions, dialogue, and SFX are intended for post-lettering.

## Page 1

Ishmael’s melancholy cure leads him waterward, decides to ship before the mast, and reaches wintry New Bedford.

### Panel 1.1
- **Graphic Beats:** B1
- **Caption:** Call me Ishmael. Whenever I find myself growing grim about the mouth... I quietly take to the ship.
- **SFX:** whss
- **Lettering Notes:** Two sentences in one caption box; keep line breaks compact.

### Panel 1.2
- **Graphic Beats:** B2
- **Caption:** Right and left, the streets take you waterward.
- **Lettering Notes:** Short aphorism; center it above the crowd.

### Panel 1.3
- **Graphic Beats:** B2
- **Caption:** Meditation and water are wedded for ever.
- **Lettering Notes:** Keep caption light and airy, not covering faces.

### Panel 1.4
- **Graphic Beats:** B3
- **Caption:** I always go to sea as a sailor, because they make a point of paying me.
- **SFX:** cree-eak
- **Lettering Notes:** One clean line; avoid covering hands with coin.

### Panel 1.5
- **Graphic Beats:** B4
- **Caption:** Saturday night in December, with my old carpet-bag—waiting a Nantucket craft.
- **Lettering Notes:** Single caption; keep it succinct.

## Page 5

Panic in the dark—ember flare, bed-leap, shout—and the landlord’s mediation ends in a courteous accord and deep sleep.

### Panel 5.1
- **Graphic Beats:** B18
- **Caption:** Then he lit his tomahawk-pipe, snuffed the light, and sprang into the bed.
- **Dialogue:** Queequeg: Who-e debel you?—you no speak-e, dam-me, I kill-e.
- **SFX:** pff
- **Lettering Notes:** Keep the caption away from the flare; set Queequeg’s balloon near him.

### Panel 5.2

### Panel 5.3

### Panel 5.4
- **Graphic Beats:** B19, B20
- **Caption:** At a word or two plain, he understood.

### Panel 5.5
- **Graphic Beats:** B20
- **Caption:** No smoking in bed; not insured. I turned in, and never slept better in my life.
- **Lettering Notes:** Two sentences in one caption; soft edges to suit the calm.

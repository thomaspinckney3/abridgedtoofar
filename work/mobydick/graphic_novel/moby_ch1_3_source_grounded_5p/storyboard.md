## Scope

From Ishmael’s opening reflection and decision to go to sea through his arrival in New Bedford, entry into the Spouter-Inn, the landlord’s ‘harpooneer’ arrangement, the Grampus crew’s arrival and Bulkington cameo, Ishmael’s uneasy night preparations, Queequeg’s return and idol ritual, the bed panic, and the final accord to share the bed.

## Style Bible

- **Visual Period:** 1840s New York and New Bedford seaport winter; tarred rigging, low-beamed tavern, whalemen’s gear, rough-hewn room furnishings.
- **Line And Color:** Crisp ink line with simplified anatomy; muted winter palette—cold slates, salt-grays, tar blacks—with candlelit ochres and ember reds for interior warmth beats.
- **Lighting:** Outdoors: flat, overcast cold. Spouter-Inn public room: dim, sooty pools of lamplight. Bedroom: single-candle chiaroscuro; later near-black with ember flare; final panel soft, restful gloom.
- **Lettering:** Narration in Ishmael’s first-person caption boxes; one caption per panel. Quoted speech appears as dialogue balloons only when the speaker is clearly visible. Keep dialect faithful but legible. Avoid crowding art; maintain reserved negative space per panel.
- **Recurring Motifs:** Breath fog in cold air; Rigging silhouettes and jawbone arches; Harpoon and bone hooks as room accents; A thin sliver of light under a door; Tomahawk-pipe ember glow

## Characters

- **Ishmael:** visual anchor: Lean young man in a threadbare dark coat, seaman’s cap; observant eyes; carries a worn carpet-bag once he travels.; costume: Wool coat, scarf, cheap gloves; later, undershirt in the room and then bedclothes.; expression range: Wry, curious, wary, startled panic, then relieved calm.; continuity note: Keep him as physical POV driver: walking waterward, counting coins, negotiating, trying the bench, in bed, then accepting Queequeg. - **Peter Coffin (landlord):** visual anchor: Gaunt, grinning innkeeper with a seaman’s stoop and ready hands.; costume: Apron or rough waistcoat; later appears with a stubby candle and a jack-knife for whittling.; expression range: Cheerfully practical, teasing, then calm mediator.; continuity note: Introduce him at the bar/public room; later lead Ishmael upstairs; finally burst in with a light to mediate.
- **Queequeg:** visual anchor: Powerful, tattooed torso; bald scalp-knot; calm bearing; beaver hat and seaman’s gear; distinctive tomahawk-pipe.; costume: Sea jacket/poncho-like shaggy cloak; later bare-torsoed during unpacking/ritual; simple trousers.; expression range: Mostly composed and methodical; brief flare of alarm in the dark; kind and courteous at accord.; continuity note: Handle the preserved head discreetly (bagged); perform small idol ritual at the fireplace; smoke the tomahawk-pipe; set it aside upon Ishmael’s boundary; invite sharing the bed kindly.
- **Bulkington:** visual anchor: Tall, broad-shouldered Southerner with a quiet, steady face; stands apart from the riot.; costume: Heavy watch-coat, comforter, icicled beard.; expression range: Reserved, intent; slips away without fuss.; continuity note: Appears briefly only in the Grampus crew entrance; show him slipping out as others call his name.

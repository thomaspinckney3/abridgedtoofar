Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Do not add motifs, props, animals, objects, or symbols just because they appear elsewhere in the book.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- Treat these as rendering/style directions only, not as a content inventory. Panel art prompts control what appears.
- visual period: 1840s New York and New Bedford seaport winter; tarred rigging, low-beamed tavern, whalemen’s gear, rough-hewn room furnishings.
- line and color: Crisp ink line with simplified anatomy; muted winter palette—cold slates, salt-grays, tar blacks—with candlelit ochres and ember reds for interior warmth beats.
- lighting: Outdoors: flat, overcast cold. Spouter-Inn public room: dim, sooty pools of lamplight. Bedroom: single-candle chiaroscuro; later near-black with ember flare; final panel soft, restful gloom.

Character continuity:
- Ishmael: Lean young man in a threadbare dark coat, seaman’s cap; observant eyes; carries a worn carpet-bag once he travels. Costume: Wool coat, scarf, cheap gloves; later, undershirt in the room and then bedclothes. Continuity: Keep him as physical POV driver: walking waterward, counting coins, negotiating, trying the bench, in bed, then accepting Queequeg. Carpet-bag appears on arrival and later by the sea-chest.
- Peter Coffin (landlord): Gaunt, grinning innkeeper with a seaman’s stoop and ready hands. Costume: Apron or rough waistcoat; later appears with a stubby candle and a jack-knife for whittling. Continuity: Introduce him at the bar/public room; later lead Ishmael upstairs; finally burst in with a light to mediate.
- Queequeg: Powerful, tattooed torso; bald scalp-knot; calm bearing; beaver hat and seaman’s gear; distinctive tomahawk-pipe. Costume: Sea jacket/poncho-like shaggy cloak; later bare-torsoed during unpacking/ritual; simple trousers. Continuity: Handle the preserved head discreetly (bagged); perform small idol ritual at the fireplace; smoke the tomahawk-pipe; set it aside upon Ishmael’s boundary; invite sharing the bed kindly.
- Bulkington: Tall, broad-shouldered Southerner with a quiet, steady face; stands apart from the riot. Costume: Heavy watch-coat, comforter, icicled beard. Continuity: Appears briefly only in the Grampus crew entrance; show him slipping out as others call his name.

Page 3 summary: The Grampus crew’s noisy return and Bulkington’s aloof exit; Ishmael’s failed bench plan and the ‘selling his head’ yarn.

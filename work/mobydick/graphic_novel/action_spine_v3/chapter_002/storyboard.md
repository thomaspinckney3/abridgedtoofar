## Action-Oriented Synopsis

I arrived in New Bedford on a cold Saturday night and learned the Nantucket packet had sailed, leaving me to wait until Monday. With little money and no acquaintances, I resolved to find cheap lodgings and not be too particular about them. I passed the bright, jolly inns as too expensive and headed down the waterward streets for cheaper quarters. Entering a low building by mistake, I stumbled through ashes into a crowded church and immediately backed out. Near the docks I found the Spouter Inn's creaking sign and chose it as my lodging for the night.

## Caption Spine

2. Sounding my pockets and finding only a few pieces of silver, I told myself to inquire the price and not be too particular where I lodged.
3. I left the Crossed Harpoons and the glowing Sword-Fish behind—too expensive and jolly—and turned waterward for cheaper inns.
4. Blundering into a public-looking doorway, I stumbled over an ash-box, choked, opened the inner door on a thundering preacher and his flock, and straightway backed out.
5. Then I saw the swinging sign with a tall jet of spray—ominous names or no—and judged this the very spot for cheap lodgings, so I made for the door.

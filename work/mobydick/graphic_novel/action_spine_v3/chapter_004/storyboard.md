# The Counterpane — Action Spine (Frames 3–8 range)

Source chapter: Moby-Dick, Ch. 4: The Counterpane

Adaptation principle: Keep the spine on waking, hazard discovered, rousing, courtesy offered, observed dressing, a decency prompt, and the harpoon shave/exit; fold the childhood phantom-hand memory and similes into captions, not into separate frames.

## Character Continuity

- Ishmael (Narrator; lodging sailor at the Spouter-Inn): Young adult man, lean build, dark wavy hair to the ears, clean but travel-worn look; in a simple off-white 19th-century nightshirt when in bed.
- Queequeg (Harpooner; Ishmael’s bedfellow): Powerfully built man with dark brown skin, dense black geometric tattoos covering arms and chest, short black hair; calm, self-possessed demeanor. When dressing: a very tall black felt beaver hat; later cowhide boots, pantaloons, waistcoat, and a heavy short pilot monkey jacket; carries a whaling harpoon.

- patchwork_counterpane: A thick patchwork quilt with many small, irregular squares and triangles in mixed colors spread over the bed. Avoid confusion: It is a quilt on the bed, not a rug or wall hanging.
- tomahawk-pipe: A small wooden smoking pipe with a hatchet-shaped metal head resting near Queequeg. Avoid confusion: Do not depict a large battle axe; it is a pipe with a hatchet-like head.
- whaling_harpoon: A stout whaling harpoon with a detachable wooden stock and a long, straight-edged steel head kept very sharp. Avoid confusion: Not a spear or pike; show the detachable stock and the steel head used for shaving when unsheathed.
- beaver_hat: A very tall black felt stovepipe-style top hat. Avoid confusion: Do not depict the animal; it is a hat of beaver felt style.

- pilot_monkey_jacket: A short, heavy, double-breasted, dark wool sailor’s jacket suited to cold weather. Avoid confusion: Not a tailcoat or long overcoat.

## Action-Oriented Synopsis

Ishmael wakes at daylight to find Queequeg’s tattooed arm hugging him, so blended with the patchwork counterpane that only its weight reveals it. He tries to free himself and rouse Queequeg and, rolling, discovers the tomahawk-pipe lying by the sleeper’s side. By much wriggling and loud expostulations he wakes Queequeg, who draws back his arm and sits up staring while Ishmael watches without misgiving. Queequeg makes a courteous sign that he will dress first and then leave Ishmael privacy to dress. Ishmael watches Queequeg dress in outlandish order—putting on his tall beaver hat first and booting himself under the bed before limping about. Concerned about the uncurtained window, Ishmael begs him to hasten into his pantaloons, and Queequeg complies and washes only his chest, arms, and hands. Ishmael sees Queequeg shave with the head of his harpoon and then march out wrapped in his pilot monkey jacket, sporting the harpoon like a marshal’s baton.

## Caption Spine

1. I woke at daylight to find Queequeg’s tattooed arm thrown over me, so blended with the patchwork counterpane that only its weight told me he was hugging me.
2. I tried to free myself and rouse him, rolled, felt a slight scratch, and, throwing aside the counterpane, I saw his tomahawk-pipe lying by his side.
3. By much wriggling and loud expostulations I woke him; he drew back his arm, shook himself, and sat up staring, while I lay quiet and curious rather than afraid.
4. He made signs that he would dress first and then leave me to dress alone—a very civilized overture I accepted as I watched him.
5. I watched him begin at the top with his very tall beaver hat, hunt up his boots, crawl under the bed to pull them on, and limp creaking about the room.
6. Seeing there were no curtains and the house opposite looked in, I begged him to hasten into his pantaloons, whereupon he complied and washed only his chest, arms, and hands.
7. Then I saw him coolly shave with the head of his harpoon, and, his toilet complete, he marched out in his pilot monkey jacket, sporting the harpoon like a marshal’s baton.

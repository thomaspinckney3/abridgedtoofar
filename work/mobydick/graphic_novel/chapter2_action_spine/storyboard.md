# Moby Dick Chapter 2 Action Spine Storyboard

Source chapter: Chapter 2, "The Carpet-Bag"

## Action-Oriented Synopsis

Ishmael packs his carpet-bag and reaches New Bedford after the Nantucket packet has already gone. Because he still wants a Nantucket whaler, he must pass two cold nights and a day in New Bedford on very little money. He rejects the warmer, brighter inns as too expensive and follows the darker streets toward the waterfront. He mistakenly enters a church, realizes it is no lodging house, and backs out. He finds the poor, creaking Spouter-Inn, accepts the omen of Peter Coffin's name, and goes in.

## Caption Spine

1. With my old carpet-bag under my arm, I came to New Bedford on a black December Saturday, too late for the Nantucket packet.
2. My mind was made up for a Nantucket craft, so I had a night, a day, and another night to eat and sleep as cheaply as I could.
3. With only a few pieces of silver in my pocket, I passed the bright, jolly inns and kept waterward.
4. In the dark streets I stumbled into a smoky church, found no bed there, and backed out again.
5. At last I found the creaking Spouter-Inn, with Peter Coffin's name beneath the sign, and took it for the cheap spot I needed.
6. No more blubbering; I scraped the ice from my frosted feet and went in to see what sort of place this Spouter might be.

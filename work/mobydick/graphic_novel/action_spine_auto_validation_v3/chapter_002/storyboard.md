# Moby-Dick Ch. 2 – The Carpet-Bag: Toward the Spouter Inn

Source chapter: Chapter 2. The Carpet-Bag

Adaptation principle: Cut the digressions and keep the walk: arrival, missed boat, frugal resolve, rejected inns, wrong-door church, and choosing the Spouter Inn. Fold background and tone into the first-person captions while staging only on-the-ground actions.

## Action-Oriented Synopsis

Ishmael arrives in New Bedford on a bitter Saturday night, learns the Nantucket packet has sailed until Monday, and decides to find cheap lodgings. He passes lively but costly inns and heads waterward. Entering a low building, he stumbles into a church mid-sermon and hastily retreats. He then finds the creaking sign of the Spouter Inn and resolves to go in and warm himself until he can sail.

## Caption Spine

1. I stuffed a shirt or two into my old carpet-bag and reached New Bedford on a bitter Saturday night, only to learn the Nantucket packet was gone till Monday.
2. With only a few pieces of silver and no friend in the place, I resolved to find cheap lodgings and not be too particular.
3. I passed the jolly, too-expensive inns and pushed waterward, my patched boots smarting in the frost.
4. In the dreary waterfront streets I entered a low, open building and stumbled over an ash-box in the porch.
5. Finding myself in a church mid-sermon on darkness and doom, I muttered and backed out.
6. At last I came to the Spouter Inn under a creaking sign—Peter Coffin—ominous but cheap-looking; I scraped the ice from my feet and made for the door to see what sort of place it might be.

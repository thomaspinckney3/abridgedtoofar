education/007 · segment 007 · confirmed

Traveling companions disappear before 'the boys'

At Eaton Hall the boys were awed, as Thackeray or Dickens would have felt in the presence of a Duke.

Critic: A reader is mildly confused: the draft cut the companions who sailed with him, so "the boys" (and later "his friends" in Berlin) has no antecedent, though the sense is still roughly inferable.

Review: The source and initial draft explicitly say several college companions sailed with Adams. The revision removes that introduction but retains 'the boys' at Eaton Hall and later 'his friends'. This is a real, small continuity break introduced by the length-reduction pass.

Proposed follow-up: Restore the short source sentence introducing the college companions near the sailing sentence, then compensate with an unrelated optional cut.

Evidence and raw result
  • ../education/007/initial.md: Several of his college companions sailed with him
  • ../education/007/draft.md: At Eaton Hall the boys were awed

education/027 · segment 027 · possible

'All were occult' loses its immediate comparison

Adams knew nothing about any of it, but though all were occult, all reacted on his mind, and he rather inclined to think the Virgin easiest to handle.

Critic: The comparison with radium and other radiant matter was cut, so 'all were occult' has no clear plural antecedent in the preceding draft and the 'easiest to handle' comparison loses its other terms, which is a real referential gap.

Review: The local comparison with radium and other radiant matter was already absent in the initial draft. The second pass further cuts the list of energies' properties and changes 'any of them' to singular 'any of it', while retaining 'all were occult' and 'the Virgin easiest to handle'. This weakens the local referent. It is less conclusive than the Berlin finding: radium, rays, and 'both energies' still appear earlier, so a reader can recover the broad comparison from the chapter.

Proposed follow-up: Clarify the compared forces with a short source-grounded link. Count this as a possible local clarity repair, not proven loss of the whole argument.

  • ../education/027/draft.md: Adams knew nothing about any of it, but though all were occult

Method and limits

The existing forage.coherence.run_coherence stage and critic_coherence prompt ran unchanged. The recording transport saved all eight complete responses and token usage, disabled automatic retries, and checked each request against its frozen preflight input.

The old production cut lists were not supplied because these revisions used new preservation plans. The prompt's conditional exemption for seams caused by authorized cuts was therefore inactive. No draft was repaired or published.

This critic checks structural clarity, not style preservation or exhaustive fidelity. It sees only an adjacent pair; earlier context may change a finding. The manual review above is separate from the model's raw output.

Method, completion and limits

The broader critic compared source, first draft and revision across causality, references, dialogue, qualifications, viewpoint, voice, interpretation, and planned dependencies. No earlier critic conclusions were supplied. All four chapters received a complete review. The two initial Adams requests reached their 16,000-token output cap; their complete finding objects were retained, but the responses were not counted as complete reviews. Two compact reviews with explicit medium effort completed the work. These retained all central texts and partner drafts, with cited source excerpts for context chapters. Six paid attempts cost $1.64, within the $2 ceiling.

The coding assistant checked the evidence and separated confirmed problems, qualified recommendations, and optional losses. This is a four-chapter, unblinded review, not proof of whole-book correctness. No prose was repaired or published. The later .env change routes future Forage text stages to Opus 5.5; these completed reviews used Sonnet 5.5.

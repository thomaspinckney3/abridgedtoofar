Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- visual period: Early 1920s America with flashbacks to the 1910s Midwest
- line and color: Clean ink lines with selective hatching; muted, slightly desaturated palette with warm sepias for memory and cooler neutrals for present reflections
- lighting: Bright, crisp spring light for the move East; soft, interior daylight for domestic moments
- recurring motifs: porch railings and straight lines (order/reserve); train platforms and suitcases (transit between East/West); seasonal foliage: spring blossoms for new start; windows and thresholds (observation vs participation)

Character continuity:
- Nick Carraway: Lean, tidy side-parted hair, watchful eyes; often framed slightly apart as an observer Costume: Conservative dark three-piece suit, white shirt, slim tie; hat and overcoat when outdoors; modest shirtsleeves at home Continuity: Narrator situated in a modest West Egg bungalow; this page places his home at the tip of the Egg and contrasts it with neighboring mansions.
- Nick's Father: Middle-aged gentleman with neatly trimmed mustache and steady gaze Costume: Early 1910s–1920s Midwestern attire: shirt, vest, jacket; pocket watch chain visible Continuity: Appears in memories and moments of guidance/support; presence signals Nick’s moral frame and practical support
- Jay Gatsby (glimpsed): Elegant male silhouette or distant figure implied by the immaculate estate; features not revealed Costume: Suggested immaculate tailoring through clean, poised silhouette if shown at all Continuity: Identified only by the mansion to Nick’s right; avoid concrete identifiers—keep Gatsby as an idea more than a person.
- Young Man at the Office: Clean-cut clerk in shirtsleeves and tie, energetic posture Costume: 1920s office wear: white shirt, suspenders, narrow tie, sleeve garters Continuity: Colleague who proposes sharing a house in a commuting town
- Finnish Woman: Sturdy, middle-aged with hair in a bun or kerchief; practical, focused demeanor Costume: Plain 1920s house dress with apron; sensible shoes Continuity: Housekeeper who makes Nick’s bed and cooks breakfast; often near an electric stove
- Man on the Road: Youngish newcomer with suitcase, slightly disoriented posture, hat in hand Costume: Simple 1920s travel wear: light suit, tie, soft hat; scuffed shoes from travel Continuity: Recent arrival who asks Nick for directions, triggering Nick’s sense of belonging

Page 1 summary: Nick recalls his father's counsel and how it formed his habit of reserving judgment, the confidences it drew to him, his guarded tolerance with limits, and his return from the East desiring moral order.

Panels:

Panel 1:
Beat: In youth, Nick receives his father's enduring advice, shaping his outlook.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Early 1910s Midwestern front porch in late afternoon. Young Nick sits on the porch steps, attentive, while his father leans on a porch post, speaking gently. Wood railings cast orderly shadows across the floorboards. A quiet yard beyond: trimmed grass, a gravel lane, a distant treeline. Father’s posture calm and instructive; Nick open, thoughtful. Warm golden light, soft breeze suggested by a lightly stirring curtain inside the open doorway. Leave clear sky and porch wall space for lettering.

Panel 2:
Beat: Nick’s reserved judgment invites confidences, from college through young adulthood, often against his will.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Early 1910s college dormitory room at night. A single shaded desk lamp pools light. Nick lies on a narrow iron-framed bed, eyes half-closed as if feigning sleep. At the edge of the bed, a rumpled, agitated classmate perches, leaning forward as if on the verge of confession. Trunks, a few campus pennants, books stacked neatly near Nick, a jacket hanging from a hook. The room’s darkness fades into corners, leaving negative space along the wall and ceiling for multiple caption boxes.

Panel 3:
Beat: Philosophy: reserving judgment as hope, tempered by class-aware caution.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Daytime train platform in a modest Midwestern town. Nick stands slightly apart, hands in coat pockets, watching a small cross-section of people pass: a well-dressed businessman, a laborer with a lunch pail, a mother with a child. Subtle spatial separation underscores observation without intrusion. Clear sky and station wall provide open areas for captions.

Panel 4:
Beat: Despite tolerance, Nick admits a firm personal limit.
Shot: close
Art prompt: Interior, evening. Nick sits at a small writing desk near a window, jaw set, eyes steady. A fountain pen rests beside a closed notebook; ashtray with a just-snubbed cigarette. Streetlights glow outside in soft bokeh. Composition leaves a swath of negative space over the desktop and window frame for captions.

Panel 5:
Beat: After returning from the East, Nick longs for order and no more tumultuous confidences.
Shot: establishing
Art prompt: Autumn afternoon outside a small Midwestern train station. Nick steps onto the platform with a worn suitcase, paused mid-step, looking toward a quiet street of neat houses and straight-lined fences. Trees with copper leaves; a breeze sends a few across the platform. The horizon and station facade leave generous negative space for the closing caption.

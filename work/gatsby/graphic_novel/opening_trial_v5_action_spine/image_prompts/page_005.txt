Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- visual period: Early 1920s America with brief flashbacks to Midwestern 1900s-1910s and college years
- line and color: Clean, expressive linework with restrained, naturalistic color; present-day scenes in neutral tones, flashbacks with a soft sepia wash
- lighting: Present: warm desk-lamp pools of light against evening shadows; Flashbacks: bright spring-summer sunlight with soft sepia tint, deep greens and sky-blues muted by age-wash
- recurring motifs: Fountain pen and open notebook; Pool of lamplight on a wooden desk; Porch swing and summer cicada dusk; College pennants and radiator pipes; Cigarette smoke curling in quiet rooms

Character continuity:
- Nick Carraway: Slim Midwesterner, neatly side-parted brown hair, observant eyes Costume: White shirt with sleeves rolled, loosened tie, waistcoat; casual cap outdoors Continuity: Remain in 1922 flashback along the West Egg shoreline by his bungalow; sepia-warm memory palette. Keep his notebook present as a recurring action anchor.
- Mr. Carraway (Nick’s Father): Tall Midwestern gentleman with tidy mustache and steady gaze Costume: Dark waistcoat, crisp white shirt, understated tie; light summer jacket on the porch Continuity: Appears briefly in flashback as a measured, composed presence.
- Office Young Man: Fresh-faced clerk in his twenties, slick hair, eager posture Costume: City office wear: shirtsleeves with suspenders, tie slightly loose Continuity: Appears only in New York office flashback to propose the commuting house.
- Finnish Woman (Housekeeper): Sturdy, middle-aged woman with a kerchief over her hair and lined, practical face Costume: Simple housedress and apron Continuity: Always near the small kitchen’s electric stove, occupied with breakfast tasks.
- Roadside Man: Nervous newcomer with hat slightly askew, uncertain stance Costume: City suit a bit rumpled from travel Continuity: Meets Nick on the country road near West Egg.

Page 5 summary: Nick walks the shoreline by his West Egg bungalow, orienting himself and us to the two Eggs across the bay; he traces his house’s position between two grand estates and reveals that the colossal place on his right belongs to Gatsby, before settling with a wry pride in his modest home’s overlooked but advantageous view.

Panels:

Panel 1:
Beat: Nick pauses at the water’s edge and studies the twin headlands across the bay, framing their likeness and difference.
Shot: establishing
Art prompt: Late-afternoon sepia sunlight over a calm bay; foreground: Nick in three-quarter back view at the shoreline, cap on, notebook in one hand as if sketching the coastline. Across the water, two similar, rounded peninsulas mirror each other, dotted with distant homes. Tall grasses ripple at Nick’s feet; gentle ripples glitter on the Sound.

Panel 2:
Beat: He turns slightly, aligning himself with his own shore—West Egg—and considers its standing against the opposite shore.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Side-profile of Nick along the West Egg shore path; behind him, newer, showy houses peep over hedges, while across the bay the opposite peninsula sits whiter and more reserved in the distance. Breeze stirs Nick’s shirtsleeves; his gaze moves from his side to the opposite shore.

Panel 3:
Beat: Nick stops where his modest bungalow sits at the very tip, visibly hemmed in by two grand estates.
Shot: establishing
Art prompt: A coastal tip of land: Nick’s small weather-beaten bungalow near the waterline, only yards from the Sound. On either side rise two sprawling estates beyond hedges and gates. A narrow path leads from Nick’s porch to the shore; a small skiff is pulled up nearby.

Panel 4:
Beat: He faces right, taking in the colossal, freshly built mansion with its tower, ivy, marble pool, and sweeping lawns.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Right of Nick’s bungalow: a towering, Normandy-style mansion with an asymmetrical stone tower, pale stone walls still sharp-edged, a sparse beard of raw ivy starting up the facade. In the foreground, a gleaming marble swimming pool and formal gardens open onto an expansive lawn.

Panel 5:
Beat: Back at his porch, Nick looks past a hedge to a sliver of lawn and the glitter of the Sound, wryly weighing the value of his small, overlooked place.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Nick seated on a simple porch swing, notebook resting on his knee, body angled toward the water. Through a gap in the hedge: a partial slice of the neighboring manicured lawn and, beyond, sunlit water. The bungalow’s paint is chipped but neat; late light warms the scene.

Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- visual period: Early 1920s America with brief flashbacks to Midwestern 1900s-1910s and college years
- line and color: Clean, expressive linework with restrained, naturalistic color; present-day scenes in neutral tones, flashbacks with a soft sepia wash
- lighting: Present: warm desk-lamp pools of light against evening shadows; Flashbacks: bright spring-summer sunlight with soft sepia tint, deep greens and sky-blues muted by age-wash
- recurring motifs: Fountain pen and open notebook; Pool of lamplight on a wooden desk; Porch swing and summer cicada dusk; College pennants and radiator pipes; Cigarette smoke curling in quiet rooms

Character continuity:
- Nick Carraway: Slim Midwesterner, neatly side-parted brown hair, observant eyes Costume: White shirt with sleeves rolled, loosened tie, waistcoat; casual cap outdoors Continuity: Remain in 1922 flashback along the West Egg shoreline by his bungalow; sepia-warm memory palette. Keep his notebook present as a recurring action anchor.
- Mr. Carraway (Nick’s Father): Tall Midwestern gentleman with tidy mustache and steady gaze Costume: Dark waistcoat, crisp white shirt, understated tie; light summer jacket on the porch Continuity: Appears briefly in flashback as a measured, composed presence.
- Office Young Man: Fresh-faced clerk in his twenties, slick hair, eager posture Costume: City office wear: shirtsleeves with suspenders, tie slightly loose Continuity: Appears only in New York office flashback to propose the commuting house.
- Finnish Woman (Housekeeper): Sturdy, middle-aged woman with a kerchief over her hair and lined, practical face Costume: Simple housedress and apron Continuity: Always near the small kitchen’s electric stove, occupied with breakfast tasks.
- Roadside Man: Nervous newcomer with hat slightly askew, uncertain stance Costume: City suit a bit rumpled from travel Continuity: Meets Nick on the country road near West Egg.

Page 1 summary: Nick sits at a modest desk and begins to write; as he does, he recalls his father’s counsel about withholding judgment and how that habit shaped him through college confessions and cautious hope.

Panels:

Panel 1:
Beat: Nick settles at his desk to begin writing, his father’s old advice foremost in his mind.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Interior, modest 1920s room at dusk. Nick Carraway sits at a small wooden desk by a window, sleeves rolled, fountain pen poised over an open notebook. A brass desk lamp casts a warm circle of light; outside, the sky is deepening blue. An ashtray and a single unlit cigarette rest nearby. Nick’s posture is attentive, reflective.

Panel 2:
Beat: Flashback: Teen Nick on a porch with his father; the rest is conveyed in their quiet understanding.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Midwestern front porch in late-afternoon summer light. A porch swing, white-painted railings, a neat yard. Mr. Carraway, dignified in a summer jacket, stands by the rail, hands loosely clasped. Teen Nick sits on the swing, looking up, attentive. They share a small, knowing nod; no one is speaking. Long shadows, cicada-still air.

Panel 3:
Beat: Back at the desk, Nick writes, acknowledging how withholding judgment shaped his dealings with people.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Return to the 1920s room. Over-the-shoulder view of Nick writing in the notebook by lamplight; steam from a nearby tea cup. His brow is slightly furrowed but calm. The room is orderly: a coat on a hook, a window cracked open to the evening.

Panel 4:
Beat: College memory: men confide in Nick, his neutrality earning him both trust and the label of ‘politician.’
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Early 1910s college common room at night: worn leather chairs, radiator, scattered books and pennants. Nick sits slightly apart, attentive as a disheveled classmate, eyes rimmed red, leans in to unburden himself. Other students linger in background, one giving Nick an appraising, half-suspicious glance.

Panel 5:
Beat: Dormitory memory: Nick feigns sleep to dodge another impending confession, yet affirms his hopeful restraint.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Dim college dorm room at night: iron bedstead, wool blanket, coat hung on a hook, moonlight through a window. Nick lies on his side, eyes closed, one hand relaxed on the blanket in studied stillness. A roommate hovers in the doorway, hesitant, on the verge of speaking.

Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- visual period: Early 1920s America with brief flashbacks to Midwestern 1900s-1910s and college years
- line and color: Clean, expressive linework with restrained, naturalistic color; present-day scenes in neutral tones, flashbacks with a soft sepia wash
- lighting: Present: warm desk-lamp pools of light against evening shadows; Flashbacks: bright spring-summer sunlight with soft sepia tint, deep greens and sky-blues muted by age-wash
- recurring motifs: Fountain pen and open notebook; Pool of lamplight on a wooden desk; Porch swing and summer cicada dusk; College pennants and radiator pipes; Cigarette smoke curling in quiet rooms

Character continuity:
- Nick Carraway: Slim Midwesterner, neatly side-parted brown hair, observant eyes Costume: White shirt with sleeves rolled, loosened tie, waistcoat; casual cap outdoors Continuity: Remain in 1922 flashback along the West Egg shoreline by his bungalow; sepia-warm memory palette. Keep his notebook present as a recurring action anchor.
- Mr. Carraway (Nick’s Father): Tall Midwestern gentleman with tidy mustache and steady gaze Costume: Dark waistcoat, crisp white shirt, understated tie; light summer jacket on the porch Continuity: Appears briefly in flashback as a measured, composed presence.
- Office Young Man: Fresh-faced clerk in his twenties, slick hair, eager posture Costume: City office wear: shirtsleeves with suspenders, tie slightly loose Continuity: Appears only in New York office flashback to propose the commuting house.
- Finnish Woman (Housekeeper): Sturdy, middle-aged woman with a kerchief over her hair and lined, practical face Costume: Simple housedress and apron Continuity: Always near the small kitchen’s electric stove, occupied with breakfast tasks.
- Roadside Man: Nervous newcomer with hat slightly askew, uncertain stance Costume: City suit a bit rumpled from travel Continuity: Meets Nick on the country road near West Egg.

Page 3 summary: At his desk, Nick writes and recalls leaving the Midwest after the war, his father’s backing, a spring arrival in New York, and the spur-of-the-moment plan that led to a shabby bungalow—where loneliness briefly gives way to a roadside exchange as a newcomer asks him the way to West Egg. The memory moves him from decision to geography, pointing him toward the place he will soon call home.

Panels:

Panel 1:
Beat: Nick writes and recalls Yale, the war, his restlessness, and the decision to go East for the bond business.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Present-night interior: Nick at his wooden desk under a pool of lamplight, fountain pen mid-stroke over an open notebook; cigarette smoke coils upward. In sepia vignette behind him: a glimpse of a Yale graduation procession with mortarboards, and a muddy trench line with distant artillery flashes. Dust motes float in the lamplight; a framed family portrait sits on the desk’s edge.

Panel 2:
Beat: Flashback: With his father’s backing, Nick arrives in New York in spring ’22 and an office colleague suggests sharing a house in a commuting town.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Sepia: A bright spring morning in a New York brokerage office—open windows, budding trees visible outside. Nick at a desk with papers; an eager young man leans in, gesturing toward a folded newspaper as if pitching an idea. Small inset at panel corner: a Midwestern porch where Nick’s father hands him an envelope, both composed.

Panel 3:
Beat: Nick settles into a shabby bungalow alone, with only a skittish dog, an old Dodge, and a Finnish housekeeper.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Sepia: Exterior-into-interior feel. A weather-beaten, small bungalow with peeling shingles and scrubby grass; Nick’s old Dodge parked askew out front with a suitcase in the back. Through the open doorway, a modest kitchen shows a Finnish woman at an electric stove, lips moving as she mutters; at Nick’s feet a small dog on a loose lead looks ready to bolt.

Panel 4:
Beat: One quiet morning on the country road, a newcomer flags Nick down.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Sepia: A sandy country road near hedges and stunted trees; morning light. Nick in a hat walks beside the lane. A slightly rumpled man steps from a stopped motorcar, hand raised to stop him, uncertain expression on his face.

Panel 5:
Beat: The man asks for directions to West Egg; Nick points the way.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Sepia: Roadside two-shot. The man faces Nick with an open, helpless look; Nick gestures down the road toward unseen shore. Wind tugs at their jackets; the bungalow roofline is distant in the background.

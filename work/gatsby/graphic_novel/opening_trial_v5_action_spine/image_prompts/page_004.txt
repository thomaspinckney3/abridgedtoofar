Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- visual period: Early 1920s America with brief flashbacks to Midwestern 1900s-1910s and college years
- line and color: Clean, expressive linework with restrained, naturalistic color; present-day scenes in neutral tones, flashbacks with a soft sepia wash
- lighting: Present: warm desk-lamp pools of light against evening shadows; Flashbacks: bright spring-summer sunlight with soft sepia tint, deep greens and sky-blues muted by age-wash
- recurring motifs: Fountain pen and open notebook; Pool of lamplight on a wooden desk; Porch swing and summer cicada dusk; College pennants and radiator pipes; Cigarette smoke curling in quiet rooms

Character continuity:
- Nick Carraway: Slim Midwesterner, neatly side-parted brown hair, observant eyes Costume: White shirt with sleeves rolled, loosened tie, waistcoat; casual cap outdoors Continuity: Remain in 1922 flashback along the West Egg shoreline by his bungalow; sepia-warm memory palette. Keep his notebook present as a recurring action anchor.
- Mr. Carraway (Nick’s Father): Tall Midwestern gentleman with tidy mustache and steady gaze Costume: Dark waistcoat, crisp white shirt, understated tie; light summer jacket on the porch Continuity: Appears briefly in flashback as a measured, composed presence.
- Office Young Man: Fresh-faced clerk in his twenties, slick hair, eager posture Costume: City office wear: shirtsleeves with suspenders, tie slightly loose Continuity: Appears only in New York office flashback to propose the commuting house.
- Finnish Woman (Housekeeper): Sturdy, middle-aged woman with a kerchief over her hair and lined, practical face Costume: Simple housedress and apron Continuity: Always near the small kitchen’s electric stove, occupied with breakfast tasks.
- Roadside Man: Nervous newcomer with hat slightly askew, uncertain stance Costume: City suit a bit rumpled from travel Continuity: Meets Nick on the country road near West Egg.

Page 4 summary: Nick leaves the roadside encounter and walks toward his West Egg bungalow; as he settles into summer—reading and stocking up on serious books—he strides down to the bay and takes in the twin peninsulas, fixing the Eggs’ geography in his mind.

Panels:

Panel 1:
Beat: Nick continues along the quiet country road toward his bungalow, his mood lifting from the brief exchange.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Early-morning 1922 country road near the shore, hedges and budding trees casting dappled light; Nick in shirtsleeves and cap walks with an easier stride, hands in pockets, a faint smile. His small weather-beaten bungalow roofline peeks ahead between trees; the bay glints beyond. Sepia-warm wash, fresh leaves stirring in a light breeze.

Panel 2:
Beat: On the bungalow porch amid bright new leaves, Nick breathes deep and reaches for a book, feeling the summer beginning.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Front porch of Nick’s small West Egg bungalow under a burst of fresh green leaves; a porch swing, a simple table with a couple of books, sunlight pooling across floorboards. Nick leans on the porch rail, inhaling the air, then reaching to pick up a book. The bay’s sparkle just visible between trees.

Panel 3:
Beat: Inside, Nick stacks newly bought red-and-gold banking volumes by a window, his Yale past glancing through as he resolves on self-improvement.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Interior of the bungalow: a modest sitting room with a simple bookcase beside a single-paned window. Sunlight stripes the floor. Nick sets a neat stack of red-and-gold-embossed volumes onto a shelf. On the wall, a small framed college photo and a discreet Yale pennant hint at his past; a single chair by the window suggests his favored reading spot.

Panel 4:
Beat: Later, Nick strolls from his yard down toward the water, musing at the chance that placed him in this unusual community on a long island.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Path from Nick’s bungalow down to a small private strip of shoreline; lawn transitioning to pebbled edge, reeds at the margin. Nick walks toward the bay with hands in pockets, a neighboring grand estate’s hedge and distant house roofline glimpsed off to one side. Afternoon light softens the scene.

Panel 5:
Beat: At the shoreline, Nick looks out across the bay, taking in the twin peninsulas—two ‘eggs’—jutting into Long Island Sound.
Shot: establishing
Art prompt: From just behind Nick at the pebbled shore, a sweeping view across calm water: two opposing headlands curving into the Sound like matched ovals separated by a narrow bay. Sailboats dot the distance; tasteful mansions and lawns crown both shores. Low afternoon sun gilds the water; a faint city haze lies far beyond.

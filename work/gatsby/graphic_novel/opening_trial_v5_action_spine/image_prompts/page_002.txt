Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- visual period: Early 1920s America with brief flashbacks to Midwestern 1900s-1910s and college years
- line and color: Clean, expressive linework with restrained, naturalistic color; present-day scenes in neutral tones, flashbacks with a soft sepia wash
- lighting: Present: warm desk-lamp pools of light against evening shadows; Flashbacks: bright spring-summer sunlight with soft sepia tint, deep greens and sky-blues muted by age-wash
- recurring motifs: Fountain pen and open notebook; Pool of lamplight on a wooden desk; Porch swing and summer cicada dusk; College pennants and radiator pipes; Cigarette smoke curling in quiet rooms

Character continuity:
- Nick Carraway: Slim Midwesterner, neatly side-parted brown hair, observant eyes Costume: White shirt with sleeves rolled, loosened tie, waistcoat; casual cap outdoors Continuity: Remain in 1922 flashback along the West Egg shoreline by his bungalow; sepia-warm memory palette. Keep his notebook present as a recurring action anchor.
- Mr. Carraway (Nick’s Father): Tall Midwestern gentleman with tidy mustache and steady gaze Costume: Dark waistcoat, crisp white shirt, understated tie; light summer jacket on the porch Continuity: Appears briefly in flashback as a measured, composed presence.
- Office Young Man: Fresh-faced clerk in his twenties, slick hair, eager posture Costume: City office wear: shirtsleeves with suspenders, tie slightly loose Continuity: Appears only in New York office flashback to propose the commuting house.
- Finnish Woman (Housekeeper): Sturdy, middle-aged woman with a kerchief over her hair and lined, practical face Costume: Simple housedress and apron Continuity: Always near the small kitchen’s electric stove, occupied with breakfast tasks.
- Roadside Man: Nervous newcomer with hat slightly askew, uncertain stance Costume: City suit a bit rumpled from travel Continuity: Meets Nick on the country road near West Egg.

Page 2 summary: At his Midwestern writing desk at night, Nick resumes writing and admits his tolerance has limits; he recalls returning from the East last autumn, then singles out Gatsby as the lone exception and reflects on Gatsby’s extraordinary hope before closing his notebook and glancing toward signs of his established Midwestern family.

Panels:

Panel 1:
Beat: Nick, back at his desk in the present, writes and pauses as he concedes that his tolerance has a limit.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: Evening interior, Midwestern study; wooden desk with an open notebook under a warm desk lamp. Nick Carraway in rolled sleeves and waistcoat sits slightly angled to the viewer, fountain pen mid-stroke, brow furrowed. Cigarette smoke curls softly. Neutral-toned room, sturdy wooden chair and simple framed wall art. Leave negative space above the desk for caption.

Panel 2:
Beat: He remembers last autumn’s return from the East, wanting the world stern and orderly.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Sepia flashback: Midwestern train station platform in autumn. Nick steps off with a modest suitcase, coat collar up, watching a few uniformed soldiers and a policeman move through a restrained crowd. Late-day light slants through station windows; a wisp of steam drifts. Fallen leaves near the platform edge. No readable signage; leave open space for caption.

Panel 3:
Beat: Back at the desk, he writes the exception: Gatsby.
Shot: close
Art prompt: Present-day palette. Tight close on Nick leaning over the notebook, pen poised with renewed intent; lamp rim glowing in frame edge. His eyes are intent, jaw set; a shallow depth of field keeps the page unreadable. Leave room for caption across the upper negative space.

Panel 4:
Beat: Nick reflects, pen hovering, on Gatsby’s heightened sensitivity and extraordinary readiness for hope.
Shot: close
Art prompt: Close three-quarter of Nick in profile at the desk; pen hovering above the page as he stares a moment into the lamplight. Soft cigarette smoke ribboning upward; warm light yields gentle contrast on his features. Background falls into shadow, keeping the focus on his thoughtful pause. Leave negative space for a longer caption.

Panel 5:
Beat: He closes the notebook; dust drifts in the lamplight as his gaze shifts to a framed family portrait, rooting him in the Middle West.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Present-day study in warm light: Nick’s hand presses the notebook closed on the desk; floating dust motes are visible in the lamp beam. On the wall beyond, a formal family portrait in an ornate but tasteful frame; sturdy Midwestern furnishings and a darkened window hinting at a quiet street. Leave upper-left negative space for two stacked captions.

Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Do not add motifs, props, animals, objects, or symbols just because they appear elsewhere in the book.
- Reserve the explicit in-panel lettering slots listed for each panel.
- Keep those slots as simple empty background; keep faces, mouths, hands, active gestures, and key props out of them.
- Do not reserve lettering space in gutters or outside panel frames.
- Page has 4 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- Treat these as rendering/style directions only, not as a content inventory. Panel art prompts control what appears.
- visual period: Early 1920s American Jazz Age, post-WWI New York and Midwest
- line and color: Clean ink linework with muted Art Deco palette: dusky blues, warm sepia, cream, and faded gold accents
- lighting: Soft window light and lamp glow indoors; overcast melancholy outdoors; selective warm highlights on the narrator; moonlit night with a single distant green pinpoint across water

## Scope

## Style Bible

- **Visual Period:** Midwest retrospective (post-1922) contrasted with Spring–Summer 1922 Long Island; brief 1910s Yale/Great War flashes.
- **Line And Color:** Clean, expressive line; period-accurate detail. TF1 cooler/sepia undertones; TF2–TF4 warmer greens and golds with crisp coastal blues.
- **Lighting:** TF1: desk lamplight and window glow; TF2–TF4: bright spring/summer daylight; TF4 mansion views at late afternoon/dusk for contrast.
- **Lettering:** Narration in understated serif caption boxes; dialogue minimal in clean mixed-case; SFX sparing and quiet. Color cue captions: cooler tint for TF1.
- **Recurring Motifs:** Nick’s hand and notebook under lamplight; Reflections in glass and water; Fast-growing spring leaves and moving air; Red-and-gold finance book spines; Nick pointing or framing vistas with his arm; Old Dodge against manicured lawns; Norman-style tower with raw ivy

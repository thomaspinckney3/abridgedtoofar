Create one finished graphic novel page as clean, text-free art.

Hard constraints:
- No readable text anywhere.
- No speech balloons, caption boxes, labels, signs, maps, or lettering.
- Do not combine panels or remix the order.
- Keep every panel visually tied to its listed beat only.
- Leave some uncluttered negative space where lettering can be added later.
- Page has 5 panels, read left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Style:
- visual period: Early 1920s Long Island and New York: Jazz Age coastal suburbs, ferries, commuting trains, clapboard bungalows, grand neo-European estates, tailored summer suits, straw boaters, and motorcars of the era.
- line and color: Crisp, clean linework with restrained, period-evocative color: sandy beiges, bay blues, lawn greens, brick reds, gold accents. Subtle grain for memory passages; bolder color for summer moments.
- lighting: Golden-hour nostalgia for memories; cool interior lamplight for confidences; bright, breathy spring-summer daylight for West Egg; moonlit water shimmer for Gatsby motifs.
- recurring motifs: open windows and single-window framings; water and shoreline distance; dust motes in sunbeams and road wake; leaves bursting like fast-film frames; red-and-gold book spines; a delicate seismograph needle as metaphor for Gatsby’s sensitivity

Character continuity:
- Nick Carraway: Mid-20s, slim, clean-shaven, neatly parted dark hair, thoughtful eyes; often in a modest gray or tan summer suit with a straw boater or flat cap. Costume: Early-1920s light suit, white shirt, subdued tie; occasionally an overcoat in autumn memory; practical leather shoes. Continuity: Keep hairstyle consistently side-parted; favor restrained posture and hands-in-pockets when listening.
- Nick’s Father: Early 50s, dignified Midwestern gentleman with neatly trimmed mustache, hair brushed back, kind yet firm eyes. Costume: 1900s–1910s waistcoat and shirtsleeves at home; dark trousers; pocket watch chain. Continuity: Appears only in memory; render with a slight sepia wash.
- Jay Gatsby (distant presence): Early 30s, handsome and composed; seen mostly in silhouette or three-quarters, immaculate light summer suit; poised at balcony or lawn’s edge. Costume: Tailored cream or white suit, pale tie, polished shoes; never disheveled. Continuity: Keep him visually elusive—avoid full-on close-ups; emphasize reflective stances and fine details (cufflinks, clean lines).
- Finnish Housekeeper: Middle-aged woman with kerchief over hair, practical apron, sturdy build. Costume: Plain dark dress, apron, work shoes. Continuity: Often seen through kitchen doorway or window near an electric stove.
- Office Colleague: Young man, early 20s, eager face, narrow tie, sleeve garters. Costume: White shirt, vest, tie; city hat when outdoors. Continuity: Appears briefly; can be implied via empty second room and missing suitcase.
- Roadside Newcomer: Young-to-middle-aged man, a bit lost; straw hat in hand; city suit dusty from travel. Costume: Light summer suit, loosened tie, scuffed shoes. Continuity: One-time interaction; keep posture slightly hunched, map or folded paper in pocket (unreadable).

Page 1 summary: Nick recalls his father’s advice, his own guarded temperament, the limits of tolerance after the East, and the first, luminous exception: Gatsby.

Panels:

Panel 1:
Beat: Memory of father’s advice taking root in youth.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Early 1910s Midwestern front porch at dusk; a teenage boy (slim, dark side-parted hair) sits on the steps while an older gentleman with a neat mustache stands beside him, calm and paternal. Fireflies, long shadows, quiet street with maple trees. Subtle sepia tone. Leave open space in the upper sky for later captions.

Panel 2:
Beat: Father delivers the core injunction about criticism.
Shot: close
Art prompt: Close on the father’s face and raised hand, gentle but firm; warm lamplight from inside the house edges his profile; the boy’s shoulder in foreground. Background soft and unfocused. Negative space along right edge.

Panel 3:
Beat: College years: Nick’s reserve draws confidences.
Shot: medium
Art prompt: A 1910s college dormitory lounge at night; green-glass desk lamp pools light on a worn sofa. Nick, now early 20s in a vest and tie, leans back feigning sleep while a disheveled classmate perches forward, confiding with intense gesture. Books stacked, window dark with a hint of campus spire outside. Leave blank wall area for caption.

Panel 4:
Beat: After returning from the East, Nick’s tolerance hits a limit—craving order.
Shot: wide
Art prompt: Autumn city sidewalk; men in nearly identical dark suits and hats stand in rigid rows as if at attention; a streetcar passes. In foreground, Nick in an overcoat pauses, hands in pockets, expression grave. Burnt-orange leaves drift. Space above crowd for caption.

Panel 5:
Beat: Gatsby singled out—gorgeous, finely attuned to life’s promises.
Shot: establishing
Art prompt: Night on a grand bayfront terrace; a poised man in a pale suit (seen three-quarters, face subtly obscured) stands near a small brass seismograph on a side table; starlight and water glimmer beyond clipped hedges. Gentle breeze stirs. Negative space in the night sky.

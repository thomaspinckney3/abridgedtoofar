## Scope

Abridged storyboard of the novel’s opening narration covering source units [1–13] (words 1–1200): Nick’s guiding advice, his guarded temperament, first evocation of Gatsby, Nick’s Midwestern past and move East, settling in West Egg, first neighborly encounter, summer’s fresh start, the red-and-gold books, the twin Eggs, and Gatsby’s mansion beside Nick’s cottage.

## Style Bible

- **Visual Period:** Early 1920s Long Island and New York: Jazz Age coastal suburbs, ferries, commuting trains, clapboard bungalows, grand neo-European estates, tailored summer suits, straw boaters, and motorcars of the era.
- **Line And Color:** Crisp, clean linework with restrained, period-evocative color: sandy beiges, bay blues, lawn greens, brick reds, gold accents. Subtle grain for memory passages; bolder color for summer moments.
- **Lighting:** Golden-hour nostalgia for memories; cool interior lamplight for confidences; bright, breathy spring-summer daylight for West Egg; moonlit water shimmer for Gatsby motifs.
- **Lettering:** Spare caption boxes for narration; minimal, naturalistic dialogue balloons. Quiet serif for captions; understated balloons with light tails. Ample negative space planned in compositions.
- **Recurring Motifs:** open windows and single-window framings; water and shoreline distance; dust motes in sunbeams and road wake; leaves bursting like fast-film frames; red-and-gold book spines; a delicate seismograph needle as metaphor for Gatsby’s sensitivity

- **Nick’s Father:** visual anchor: Early 50s, dignified Midwestern gentleman with neatly trimmed mustache, hair brushed back, kind yet firm eyes.; costume: 1900s–1910s waistcoat and shirtsleeves at home; dark trousers; pocket watch chain.; expression range: Measured, instructive warmth; steady gaze.; continuity note: Appears only in memory; render with a slight sepia wash.
- **Jay Gatsby (distant presence):** visual anchor: Early 30s, handsome and composed; seen mostly in silhouette or three-quarters, immaculate light summer suit; poised at balcony or lawn’s edge.; costume: Tailored cream or white suit, pale tie, polished shoes; never disheveled.; expression range: Quiet, intent gaze toward the water; composed smile; aura of expectancy.; continuity note: Keep him visually elusive—avoid full-on close-ups; emphasize reflective stances and fine details (cufflinks, clean lines).

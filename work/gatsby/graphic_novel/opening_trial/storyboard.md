# The Great Gatsby — Opening Arrival Graphic Novel Experiment

- Book id: `gatsby`
- Source: `/Users/tp/abridgedtoofar/books/gatsby/abridgment.txt`
- Excerpt words: 1-1200

## Scope

From Nick’s recalled advice from his father through his settling at West Egg and first sight of Gatsby’s mansion (Chapter 1 opening excerpt).

## Style Bible

- **Visual Period:** United States, 1915–1922: Middle West to Long Island, summer 1922
- **Line And Color:** Clean, precise ink lines with restrained color; sandy neutrals and cool bay blues punctuated by warm golds and ivy greens; occasional double-exposure overlays for metaphor.
- **Lighting:** Golden hour exteriors; crisp summer sunlight; lamplit interiors with soft shadows; dusk blues over the bay.
- **Lettering:** Narration captions in elegant serif italics; dialogue in clean uppercase sans; sound effects small and unobtrusive.
- **Recurring Motifs:** Dust motes drifting in light; Seismograph needle and paper ribbon; Red-and-gold book spines; Egg shapes and shorelines; Raw ivy creeping on stone; Water of Long Island Sound shimmering at dusk

## Characters

- **Nick Carraway:** visual anchor: Slim, clean-shaven man in his mid-20s; neatly parted brown hair; observant gray-blue eyes; reserved posture.; costume: 1922 light wool suit (tan or gray), white shirt, slim tie, straw boater or flat cap as needed; in college/war flashbacks: Yale sweater or WWI doughboy uniform.; expression range: Subtle: thoughtful, politely attentive, faintly amused, quietly weary.; continuity note: Keep Nick’s hair part, lean build, and understated dress consistent; he often stands slightly apart from others, hands in pockets or holding a notebook/book.
- **Nick’s Father:** visual anchor: Older gentleman with neat silver-streaked hair and small mustache; calm, direct gaze.; costume: Dark three-piece suit with pocket watch chain; white shirt; conservative tie.; expression range: Measured warmth; gently instructive.; continuity note: Appears only in memory; keep porch setting and twilight tone consistent.
- **Confiding Young Men (composite):** visual anchor: Assorted college-age men with loosened ties or varsity sweaters; slightly haggard or intense looks.; costume: 1910s collegiate attire: shirtsleeves, vests, lettered sweaters.; expression range: Anxious, over-eager, conspiratorial.; continuity note: Use as background foils; avoid distinct anchors so they read as various ‘wild, unknown men.’
- **Finnish Housekeeper:** visual anchor: Sturdy middle-aged woman with kerchief over hair and kind, lined face; practical stance.; costume: Simple long skirt, rolled sleeves, apron; sensible shoes.; expression range: Stoic, murmuring concentration; occasional curt nod.; continuity note: Always near the stove or housekeeping tasks; include small muttered SFX rather than dialogue.
- **Direction-Seeking Man:** visual anchor: Newly arrived young man with soft hat and travel-creased suit; earnest, a bit lost.; costume: Light summer suit with loosened tie; scuffed shoes; carrying a folded timetable.; expression range: Puzzled, hopeful.; continuity note: Appears only once on the country road; keep him hat-in-hand when he asks.
- **Jay Gatsby (distant presence):** visual anchor: Handsome man mid-30s seen in silhouette or at a distance; straight posture; slicked dark hair.; costume: Evening wear or immaculate light suit depending on scene; no close facial detail yet.; expression range: Unreadable, intent (implied).; continuity note: Show him as a silhouette/figure on terrace or near water; withhold close facial features until later chapters.

## Page 1

Nick recalls his father’s advice and his habit of reserving judgment, with glimpses of college confidences and the limits of his tolerance.

### Panel 1.1 (medium)

- **Beat:** Father’s advice on withholding criticism.
- **Image Prompt:** 1910s Middle Western front porch at twilight; Nick as a teenager sits beside his father on wooden chairs; father leans forward, hand gently raised, giving counsel. Fireflies hover; a quiet street and elm trees in soft purple dusk. Period suits: father in dark three-piece, young Nick in simple shirt and suspenders. Warm lamplight from inside spills onto the porch.
- **Caption:** In my younger and more vulnerable years my father gave me advice.
- **Dialogue:** Father: Whenever you feel like criticizing anyone, remember not everyone had your advantages.
- **SFX:** CRICK-CRICK (crickets)
- **Continuity:** Establish young Nick’s look for later recognition; father’s measured tone.
- **Source Anchor:** "Whenever you feel like criticizing anyone... advantages that you've had."

### Panel 1.2 (medium)

- **Beat:** Nick’s habit of reserving judgments opens confidences—and bores.
- **Image Prompt:** 1910s college dorm common room at night; Nick, older now, sits on a threadbare couch, posture attentive but reserved; a disheveled student leans in, whispering intensely. Books and empty coffee cups litter a table; a green-shaded desk lamp casts moody pools of light.
- **Caption:** So I reserve all judgments. It opens curious natures to me—and not a few veteran bores.
- **Dialogue:** Confider: You can’t tell anyone—
- **Continuity:** Keep Nick’s calm listening pose; composite confider can vary later.
- **Source Anchor:** "I'm inclined to reserve all judgements... victim of not a few veteran bores."

### Panel 1.3 (wide)

- **Beat:** Feigning sleep to avoid another ‘intimate revelation.’
- **Image Prompt:** Dormitory bunk room; late night. Nick lies on a narrow bed, eyes closed, one arm over his face, coat hanging on a hook. Another student stands in the doorway, hesitant, about to speak. A single bare bulb glows.
- **Caption:** Often I feigned sleep when an intimate revelation quivered on the horizon.
- **Dialogue:** Confider: I just—
- **SFX:** CREAK (door)
- **Continuity:** Reinforce Nick’s avoidance without speechiness.
- **Source Anchor:** "Frequently I have feigned sleep... an intimate revelation was quivering on the horizon."

### Panel 1.4 (medium)

- **Beat:** Accused in college of being a ‘politician’ for knowing secret griefs.
- **Image Prompt:** College quad by day; Nick walks past bulletin boards while two students whisper and glance his way. Autumn leaves on the ground; a stone building backdrop.
- **Caption:** The abnormal mind attaches to this quality. They called me a politician—privy to secret griefs.
- **Dialogue:** Student (whisper): He knows everything.
- **Continuity:** Campus setting contrasts with prior interiors.
- **Source Anchor:** "I was unjustly accused of being a politician... privy to the secret griefs of wild, unknown men."

### Panel 1.5 (wide)

- **Beat:** Limits of tolerance; decencies unequally parceled.
- **Image Prompt:** City sidewalk afternoon; Nick stands still, hands in pockets, as passersby of different classes stream past: a banker in a topcoat, a laborer with lunch pail, a flapper with beads. Long shadow lines; muted colors.
- **Caption:** Decencies are parceled unequally at birth. Even tolerance has a limit.
- **Continuity:** Externalize internal thought via contrasting passersby; keep Nick centered, slightly apart.
- **Source Anchor:** "a sense of the fundamental decencies is parcelled out unequally... I come to the admission that it has a limit."

## Page 2

Nick’s post-Eastern reaction, his singular exception for Gatsby, the ‘foul dust,’ and his background leading to the move East.

### Panel 2.1 (wide)

- **Beat:** After returning last autumn, Nick longs for a world at moral attention.
- **Image Prompt:** City street in late autumn; a line of uniformed men (soldiers, police) stand at attention along a curb; Nick in civilian suit passes by, posture rigid, eyes forward. Pale sunlight; bare trees.
- **Caption:** After I came back from the East, I wanted the world in uniform—at moral attention.
- **Continuity:** Temporal cue: ‘last autumn’; cooler palette than summer scenes.
- **Source Anchor:** "I wanted the world to be in uniform and at a sort of moral attention forever."

### Panel 2.2 (establishing)

- **Beat:** Only Gatsby is exempt; he both offends and fascinates.
- **Image Prompt:** Night on Long Island Sound; distant silhouette of a man (Gatsby) on a terrace above dark lawns, looking across the bay; soft party lights behind him; the water glimmers.
- **Caption:** Only Gatsby was exempt—he represented what I scorn, yet there was something gorgeous about him.
- **Continuity:** Do not reveal Gatsby’s face; emphasize distance and allure.
- **Source Anchor:** "Only Gatsby... was exempt... there was something gorgeous about him."

### Panel 2.3 (detail)

- **Beat:** Gatsby’s sensitivity like a machine registering distant earthquakes.
- **Image Prompt:** Close on an early 20th-century seismograph: brass arm tapping a moving paper ribbon. Double-exposure overlay: faint profile of Gatsby’s silhouette aligned with the needle’s tremor.
- **Caption:** A heightened sensitivity—an extraordinary gift for hope.
- **SFX:** TICK…TICK…
- **Continuity:** Introduce the seismograph motif for reuse; keep overlay subtle.
- **Source Anchor:** "as if he were related to one of those intricate machines that register earthquakes... an extraordinary gift for hope."

### Panel 2.4 (wide)

- **Beat:** ‘Foul dust’ in the wake of Gatsby’s dreams.
- **Image Prompt:** Dusk over the bay; a small motorboat’s wake ripples toward shore; in the air above the water, dust-like motes swirl in the last light, mingled with stray confetti.
- **Caption:** He turned out all right. It was the foul dust in the wake of his dreams that closed my interest in short-winded elations.
- **SFX:** RRRR (distant motor)
- **Continuity:** Set up ‘dust’ as a recurring visual; keep colors somber blue-violets.
- **Source Anchor:** "what foul dust floated in the wake of his dreams... closed out my interest..."

### Panel 2.5 (wide)

- **Beat:** Background: Middle West, Yale, the war; restlessness and decision to go East.
- **Image Prompt:** Triptych montage: 1) Formal family portrait in a Middle Western parlor; 2) Yale 1915 diploma with mortarboard on a table; 3) Nick in WWI uniform crouched in a trench; 4) Seamless into a spring 1922 train roaring east past green fields.
- **Caption:** Prominent Middle West. Yale ’15. The war. Restless—I went East to learn the bond business.
- **SFX:** CLACK-CLACK (train)
- **Continuity:** Anchor dates subtly (1915, spring ’22) via props; keep Nick identifiable across vignettes.
- **Source Anchor:** "My family have been prominent... Yale in 1915... the Great War... so I decided to go East and learn the bond business."

## Page 3

Nick’s West Egg setup: the bungalow, Finnish housekeeper, brief dog, first neighborly exchange, summer’s fresh start, and the Eggs—culminating in Gatsby’s colossal mansion beside Nick’s small house.

### Panel 3.1 (establishing)

- **Beat:** The weather-beaten bungalow at eighty a month; roommate sent to Washington.
- **Image Prompt:** Morning in a quiet coastal commuting town; a small weathered shingle bungalow with a sagging porch near the water; wild grass; a paper tacked inside the window (rent $80/month). Nick arrives with a suitcase; a second suitcase sits abandoned on the porch with a telegram atop: ‘Ordered to Washington.’
- **Caption:** A weather-beaten cardboard bungalow at eighty a month. My would-be roommate went to Washington; I went alone.
- **SFX:** GULLS
- **Continuity:** Place the house near visible water to foreshadow proximity to the Sound.
- **Source Anchor:** "a weather-beaten cardboard bungalow at eighty a month... the firm ordered him to Washington, and I went out... alone."

### Panel 3.2 (medium)

- **Beat:** A few-day dog, old Dodge, and the Finnish woman at the electric stove.
- **Image Prompt:** Cozy kitchen interior; morning sun; the Finnish housekeeper stirs a pan on an electric stove, muttering. Nick sits at a small table with coffee; on a wall hook hangs an empty dog leash and collar. Through a window, an old Dodge car is parked in a sandy drive.
- **Caption:** I had a dog—for a few days—an old Dodge, and a Finnish woman who cooked and murmured wisdom to herself over the electric stove.
- **SFX:** HUM (stove), MUTTER MUTTER
- **Continuity:** Keep the Dodge’s silhouette consistent in later exterior shots.
- **Source Anchor:** "I had a dog—at least I had him for a few days... and a Finnish woman... muttered... over the electric stove."

### Panel 3.3 (medium)

- **Beat:** A newer arrival asks Nick the way to West Egg village.
- **Image Prompt:** Sunny country road lined with hedges and new leaves; the Direction-Seeking Man, hat in hand, looks helpless; Nick gestures down the road, relaxed now. A glimpse of bay water at the turn ahead.
- **Caption:** Lonely, until a man newer than I stopped me on the road.
- **Dialogue:** Stranger: How do you get to West Egg village? / Nick: Straight on, then left by the Sound.
- **Continuity:** Establish neighborly tone; same road returns visually in later Long Island sequences.
- **Source Anchor:** "'How do you get to West Egg village?' he asked helplessly."

### Panel 3.4 (detail)

- **Beat:** Summer’s restart; red-and-gold books like new money.
- **Image Prompt:** Interior shelf close-up: a neat row of a dozen red-and-gold spined volumes titled ‘Banking,’ ‘Credit,’ ‘Investment Securities’; sunbeams cut dust in the air; faint reflection of bay light. Inset: Nick at a single window, reading.
- **Caption:** With sunshine and bursting leaves, life began over again. I bought red-and-gold books—like new money from the mint.
- **SFX:** RUSTLE (leaves)
- **Continuity:** Introduce the ‘single window’ reading posture as Nick’s POV motif.
- **Source Anchor:** "bursts of leaves... life was beginning over again... I bought a dozen volumes... in red and gold like new money from the mint."

### Panel 3.5 (wide)

- **Beat:** The two Eggs and Gatsby’s colossal mansion beside Nick’s tiny house.
- **Image Prompt:** Aerial-like view of two egg-shaped peninsulas jutting into Long Island Sound under bright summer light; LEFT labeled ‘West Egg’ (less fashionable), RIGHT labeled ‘East Egg.’ Foreground lower strip: Nick’s small shingled house perched at the tip of West Egg, fifty yards from the water, squeezed between two vast estates. On the right estate: a spanking-new stone mansion imitating a Normandy town hall, with a single tower, raw ivy just starting, a marble swimming pool glinting, and sweeping lawns across forty acres.
- **Caption:** That riotous island of two enormous Eggs. I lived at West Egg—my small house pinched between vast places. To my right stood a colossal imitation of a Normandy Hôtel de Ville—Gatsby’s mansion.
- **Continuity:** Keep mansion features consistent: tower, raw ivy, marble pool, vast lawn; do not show Gatsby up close.
- **Source Anchor:** "two unusual formations... a pair of enormous eggs... I lived at West Egg... My house... squeezed between two huge places... a factual imitation of some Hôtel de Ville in Normandy... It was Gatsby's mansion."
